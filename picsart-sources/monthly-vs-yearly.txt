Monthly Subscription: Flexibility is the core of the monthly plan. It's the go-to choice for creators who want the power of a premium subscription with the freedom to adjust their plan month by month.

Yearly Subscription: Annual billing allows users to secure a year-long subscription with a one-time payment. This structure offers a cost advantage, as the total price is set lower than the sum of twelve consecutive monthly payments.

Note: With our annual subscription, the subscription page shows the equivalent monthly price along with the line "billed annually" at the bottom meaning that you'll pay for the year with one purchase.

What is the difference between the subscription plans in iTunes?
All Picsart subscriptions in iTunes provide the same core benefits, with options for different pricing and durations. Plus and Pro plans work the same way: every Pro option offers the same tools, just as every Plus option offers the same benefits.
