Credits are virtual tokens that allow you to use various generative AI tools. Each generation with these tools requires a set number of credits. Every time you use a generative tool, the corresponding number of credits is deducted from your balance.

How are credits allocated?
Credits are allocated based on your Picsart subscription plan. You can check the allocated credits count on our pricing page at picsart.com/pricing.

Can I purchase additional credits?
Yes, additional credits can be purchased if you need more than your plan provides. These add-on credits do not expire and can be rolled over to subsequent months if unused.

What happens if a generation does not complete successfully?
If a generation does not complete successfully, the credits you spent will automatically be returned to your account, ensuring your credit balance is not deducted for unsuccessful attempts.
