FAQs (on pricing page, full text):
Q: What are the benefits of a subscription and which plan should I choose?
A: Subscribing unlocks advanced editing tools, an exclusive content library, increased cloud storage, and an ad-free experience on the mobile app. Choose Pro for standard photo and video editing, or Ultra if you require heavy AI generation, premium AI models, and team collaboration features.

Q: Will I get a free trial before I subscribe?
A: Most plans include a free trial, though eligibility depends on your account history and the specific plan chosen. You will receive instant confirmation of your eligibility during the checkout process.

Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?
A: You can upgrade from Pro to Ultra at any time if purchased directly through Picsart.com. If you downgrade a plan, the new billing cycle will start after your current one expires.

Q: How can I pay for a subscription, and are there discounts available?
A: We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and Apple/Google Pay, and we do not offer installment plans. You can save up to 60% by choosing an annual subscription over a monthly one [NB: page's own toggle badge says "SAVE UP TO 40%" - minor same-page inconsistency, not scored by any codebook variable].

Q: How do I cancel my subscription or request a refund?
A: You can cancel your subscription by following the instructions here. Refunds for mobile app subscriptions are subject to Apple or Google Play Store policies, while website purchases can be requested via this link. If you're not sure where you originally purchased your subscription, you can refer to this article.

Q: What are credits and how can I get more?
A: Credits are virtual tokens used for generative AI tools. If you need more, you can upgrade to the Ultra plan or purchase "add-on" credits through your profile settings.

Q: Do unused credits roll over?
A: Monthly subscription credits expire at the end of each billing cycle and do not roll over. However, any purchased add-on credits remain in your account and will not expire until used.

Q: Will I get my credits back if a generation fails?
A: Yes, if a generation is unsuccessful, your consumed credits will be automatically rolled back to your account within 24-48 hours.

Q: Can I use my benefits across multiple devices and platforms?
A: Yes... iOS, Android, web, and the Microsoft Store...

Q: Will I still have access to my benefits if I cancel?
A: Yes, you will continue to have full access to all subscription benefits until the current billing period expires.
