Web: How do I cancel my Picsart subscription on the website?
Visit www.picsart.com and log in.
Click on your profile photo.
Select Settings.
Navigate to Billing & Subscriptions and click on Cancel subscription.
Choose Downgrade to free plan

IMPORTANT: Downgrade to Free Plan means the same as Cancel Subscription. Once you downgrade, your subscription will be canceled and your account will continue on the free plan.

Windows: How can I cancel my Picsart subscription through the Microsoft Store?
To cancel your Picsart subscription on Windows, follow the steps provided by the Microsoft Store when managing subscriptions.

IMPORTANT (mobile, from top of article not fully captured): Ensure you are logged into the app store account (Google Play or App Store) that was used to purchase the subscription. This ensures the subscription appears in your active subscriptions.
