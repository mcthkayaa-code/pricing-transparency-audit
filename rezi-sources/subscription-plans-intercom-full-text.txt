The prices shown above are our standard rates. Depending on your country or region, you may see
different pricing or special offers.
To view the exact price available to you:
1. Open Rezi.
2. Click Upgrade.
3. The pricing window will display the plans and pricing available for your location.
We always recommend checking the Upgrade page in your account for the most accurate and up-to-date
pricing."

CRITICAL FINDING: this is the ONLY document found (across pricing page, terms, and both help-doc sources)
that states a Quarterly price figure: "$19/month (billed quarterly)". This is NOT shown anywhere on the
public https://www.rezi.ai/pricing marketing page (no toggle exists there at all - only one Pro card,
$29/month, no period switcher). This help article also discloses that actual pricing may vary by region
and is only definitively shown inside the logged-in app's Upgrade flow, which the documents-only rule
prohibits accessing (would require login).
