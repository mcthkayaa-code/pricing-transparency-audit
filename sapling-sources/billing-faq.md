## Verbatim Q&A

Q: "Why was I automatically started on a subscription when signing up?"
A: "While you may have been started on a free trial, you were not subscribed. You are only
subscribed when you provide payment information at https://sapling.ai/plans. The free trial
automatically cancels if no payment method is added."
=> Direct evidence: trial does NOT require a card to start, and does NOT auto-convert to a paid
   subscription -- it auto-CANCELS absent a proactively added payment method. This is the opposite
   of silent auto-conversion.

Q: "Do you have any discounted plans?"
A: "Sapling has not offered discounted plans during specific events or seasons. However, we do
offer substantial discounts for nonprofits and educational organizations. For teams and
enterprises, higher seat tiers receive volume discounts. Please contact us with the number of
users/seats you require, and we will provide a customized estimate."

Q: "Where can I access my invoices?"
A: "Please sign in and visit https://sapling.ai/dashboard-billing and click Manage billing. There
you can view all invoices in Stripe. Please note that you must have subscribed using the same
email address you used to sign up for Sapling."

Q: "Where can I update my payment information or cancel my plan?"
A: "Please sign in and visit https://sapling.ai/dashboard-billing and click Manage billing. There
you can manage your billing information and subscription. Please note that you must have
subscribed using the same email address you used to sign up for Sapling."
=> CONFLICTS with ToS section 3.3 ("You may cancel the Subscription Service by contacting us at:
   support@sapling.ai"). This help-center page documents a self-serve dashboard/Stripe-portal
   cancellation route with no mention of contacting support. Per protocol 6.2, ToS ranks above the
   help center for contractual variables (cancellation is a contractual variable), so
   cancellation_self_serve is CODED from the ToS (contact_required); this conflict is recorded in
   conflict_note with both URLs and quotes.

Q: "What payment methods can I use?"
A: "Sapling uses Stripe for most billing transactions... For enterprise customers with a minimum
annual spend of $1,200, we can also accommodate ACH and wire-based payments."
