SPEECHIFY USAGE LIMITS
Effective Jan 30, 2026

TL;DR: For all of 2026, we're giving every Premium user up to 1,000,000 words per month -- guaranteed. Your word
count resets monthly and applies to all Premium/HD/AI voices. You can track your usage in the app. If you have a
disability or need more words for accessibility, reach out. We don't allow account sharing or using Speechify for
commercial distribution without permission.

1. Definitions
  Premium Voices = Any voice marked Premium, HD, AI, or equivalent.
  Monthly Period = Your monthly billing cycle.
  Baseline Allowance = The guaranteed minimum number of Premium Voice words per month under your plan.
  Extension = Extra Premium Voice words Speechify gives you beyond your Baseline Allowance.

2. Baseline Allowance (Guaranteed Minimum)
  "All Premium subscribers are contractually guaranteed at least: 150,000 words per month."
  "Speechify will not reduce this baseline without providing at least 30 days' advance notice."

3. Guaranteed 2026 Extension (1M Words)
  "From January 1 through December 31, 2026, Speechify guarantees all Premium subscribers a total Premium Voice
  usage limit of 1,000,000 words per month."
  - Applies to all Premium subscribers, regardless of plan type or promotion.
  - Will not be reduced for any user during 2026.
  - "Expires automatically on January 1, 2026, unless renewed or replaced by updated terms." [NOTE: this is the
    vendor's own exact wording; it is internally inconsistent with the stated "through December 31, 2026" active
    window one sentence earlier (expiry date appears to be a vendor drafting error, presumably meaning Jan 1,
    2027). Quoted verbatim, not corrected -- flagged in coder_note as an observation, not used to alter any coded
    value.]

4. Optional Extensions (After 2026)
  "Are not guaranteed," "May vary by user, location, promotion, or system capacity," "Can be changed or revoked
  at any time without notice."

5. Fair-Use and Abuse Policy
  "Speechify may pause or restrict access to Premium Voices if we reasonably believe that an account is:
   - Shared by multiple individuals,
   - Used to generate audio for resale, distribution, or broadcasting,
   - Operated by bots, scripts, or automated tools,
   - Violating our Acceptable Use Policy."
  (All triggers are CONDUCT-based -- sharing/reselling/botting/policy-violation -- not an unquantified
  volume/intensity-of-use standard. No separate "Acceptable Use Policy" document was found linked anywhere on
  the site; footer only links Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Conduct clauses of this kind are excluded
  from unquantified_limit_clause by the codebook's own rule ("a prohibition on illegal or abusive content is not
  a usage limit").)

6. Accessibility Support -- complimentary word extensions for documented accessibility needs, contact
   help@speechify.com, "Accessibility Waiver".

7. Tracking Your Usage -- "real-time word usage tracking in the app or on your dashboard."

No free-plan word/volume limit is stated anywhere on this page ("No specific limits are stated on this page" for
Free was confirmed by omission -- the page's scope is explicitly "Premium Voices" throughout).
