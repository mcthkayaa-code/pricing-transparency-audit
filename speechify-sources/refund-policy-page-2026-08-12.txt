Refund Policy for Text to Speech Subscriptions   [<- this is the entry tier's product line]
Conditions for a Refund
  Speechify will issue refunds for purchases made in the past 7 days in case of
  a) if it is determined that your account has been taken over by a third party and used fraudulently, or
  b) you have been charged by Speechify in error.
  "You must contact us within 7 days from the date of the subscription (including any trial period) for us to be
  able to issue a refund."
Unused subscription
  "Annual Subscription: If you have used no more than 500 HD words, you must contact us within 7 days of the
  payment for us to be able to issue a refund."
When a refund cannot be issued?
  - "Speechify does not offer partial refunds. If you've used the service for part of the billing cycle and then
    canceled, you will not be eligible to receive a refund for the charge"
  - "Any refund request after 7 days from the date of the subscription (including any trial period)."
  - "Subscriptions purchased on a Monthly, Quarterly, or Discounted basis are non-refundable."
  - "if you purchase a subscription through the Apple iTunes Store or our iOS application, the sale is final, and
    we will not provide a refund... subject to Apple's applicable payment policy, which also may not provide for
    refunds." Same statement for Google Play.
How to request a refund? help desk request or help@speechify.com
