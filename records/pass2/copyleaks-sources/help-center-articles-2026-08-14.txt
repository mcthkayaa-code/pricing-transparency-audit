=== URL 1: https://help.copyleaks.com/hc/en-us/articles/24287690126349-What-is-the-cancellation-policy-for-Copyleaks ===
Title: What is the cancellation policy for Copyleaks?
Category: BILLING
Answer:
You can cancel your subscription at any time.
Refunds will only cover the most recent subscription cycle. To receive a refund, you must submit a refund
request within the first 10 days of the billing cycle and no credits must have been used during that period.
Payment processing fees will not be deducted from the original price if the refund request has been
submitted within 24 hours of the payment date.
You can review our Cancellation Policy and complete Terms of Use from our page here.

=== URL 2: https://help.copyleaks.com/hc/en-us/articles/23768575396365-How-do-I-cancel-my-subscription ===
Title: How do I cancel my subscription?
Category: BILLING / Cancellations
Answer:
Cancelling your Copyleaks Subscription
To cancel your subscription:
1. Go to Settings and select Billing.
2. Click the Options dropdown menu in the top right corner and click on Cancel Subscription.
3. From here you can complete a brief survey and cancel your subscription. You will have access to your
   account until the end of the billing cycle.
Please note, you will lose access to your scan history when you cancel your subscription.
[No mention of contacting support being required — self-service flow described entirely within account
Settings > Billing.]

=== URL 3: https://help.copyleaks.com/hc/en-us/articles/23768714188173-Do-my-credits-expire-if-I-don-t-use-them-at-the-end-of-each-billing-cycle ===
Title: Do my credits expire if I don't use them at the end of each billing cycle?
Category: BILLING / Manage
Answer:
Yes, credits expire at the end of each billing cycle.
For example: if you purchase a 1 month 500 credit plan on April 10th and you only use 400 credits by May 9th,
the unused 100 credits will not roll over to the next month.
