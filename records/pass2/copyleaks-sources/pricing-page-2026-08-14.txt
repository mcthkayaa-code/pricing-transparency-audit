Q: How does Copyleaks pricing work?
A: Copyleaks offers flexible pricing based on your usage and needs. Personal and Pro plans are subscription-based and include a monthly credit allowance. 1 credit covers up to 250 words or 1 image. Choose the plan that fits your workflow, and upgrade anytime as your needs grow. Enterprise and Education plans are custom-tailored based on your organization's size, integration needs, and usage volume. These plans are designed for large teams, institutions, or districts and include advanced features, analytical resources, and support. You'll work directly with our team to build a solution that scales with your needs and goals.

Q: What happens if I exceed my monthly credit limit?
A: If you reach your monthly credit allowance on a Personal or Pro plan, you have two options: You can upgrade your plan to access more monthly credits, or purchase additional one-time credits to continue scanning without interruption. Your allowance resets at the start of each new billing cycle, and you'll receive notifications as you approach your limit. For Enterprise and Education users, scan volume is customized in advance. Our customer success team will assist in adjusting your plan accordingly.

Q: Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?
A: Yes, you can cancel or change your plan at any time, but there are a few important things to keep in mind: All plans renew automatically unless canceled before the next billing cycle. Monthly plans are not one-time purchases; your subscription will continue until you cancel it. Canceling a plan stops future renewals but does not immediately end your current access. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period. Upgrading or downgrading is possible, but Copyleaks plans don't stack. Switching plans will override your current plan, including any remaining credits. For example, changing from an annual AI-only plan to a monthly AI + Plagiarism plan will forfeit the unused annual credits. Refunds are not guaranteed upon cancellation. If you believe you're eligible, you must contact support to request a refund directly. We will only issue a refund if you submitted your request within the first 10 days of your billing cycle and did not scan any pages or use any credits from your account. We recommend reviewing your plan details and cancellation settings from your account dashboard to avoid unexpected charges. For more information about cancellation, refunds, and other common questions, please see our Terms of Use.

Q: What are my plan options?

Both plans include full access to our AI detection and plagiarism detection features for text scanning and AI detection for image scanning. For education and enterprise solutions, including API access and LMS integration, please contact our sales team for a custom pricing plan.

Q: How many words, pages, or images can I scan with each plan?
