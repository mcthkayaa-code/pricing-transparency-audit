--- FAQ (Subscription tab) ---
What are credits?
"Credits are used in-app for AI photo editing, photo generation, and other advanced AI features on Aragon. You use credits each time you use one of these features."

What are characters?
"A character is a set of 6–10 photos of the same person. You upload these photos so the AI can generate AI photos of that individual."

What's the difference between purchasing and buying credits?
"A subscription gives you a recurring monthly credit allocation plus access to premium features... Purchasing credits is a one-time top-up that adds to your existing balance, useful when you need more credits without changing your plan."

Can I use Aragon for free?
"Yes. Free users receive 16 credits per active day. For higher limits and full feature access, a paid subscription is required."

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
"Yes, at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle."

Do unused credits roll over?
"Monthly subscription credits expire at the end of each billing period and do not roll over. Additionally purchased credits do roll over and stay usable as long as your subscription is active."

Do unused characters roll over?
"Unused characters in your subscription do not roll over, but additionally purchased characters do roll over and remain usable as long as you stay subscribed."

Do my characters ever expire?
"Characters remain active as long as your subscription is active. If you cancel, your characters will expire 30 days after cancellation."

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?
"Yes, you can pause or cancel anytime from your billing page. You'll retain full access until the end of your current billing period."
