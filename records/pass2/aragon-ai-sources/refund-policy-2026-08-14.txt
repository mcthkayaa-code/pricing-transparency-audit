=== Headshot Refund Policy ("The Aragon Guarantee") ===
"Although close to 99% of our customers are satisfied with their AI headshots, there may be instances where your photos don't accurately represent you" — complimentary redo or full refund offered.

What doesn't qualify:
- Upload instructions weren't followed
- "downloading your photos is considered as using your product"
- Requests arrive after "14 calendar days of purchase"
- Team plan accounts: partial refunds for unused credits only

=== Subscription Refund Policy ===
"Monthly plans: Refund requests must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the charge"
"Annual plans: Refund requests must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the charge"
"The refund applies to your most recent payment only"
"Refunds will only be granted if no credits have been used during the billing period"
No refunds for recurring charges if subscription wasn't canceled before renewal.

=== Duplicate Charges & Unauthorized Transactions ===
Duplicate charges: full refund after verification. "Verified unauthorized charges will be refunded in full following investigation."

=== Subscription Cancellation (on this page) ===
"All subscription plans renew automatically unless canceled before the renewal date." Cancellations take effect end-of-cycle; unused portions not refunded.

=== How To Request a Refund ===
Contact support via "support@aragon.ai or using our chatbox."

=== How Long Do Refunds Take ===
"We will review and respond to refund requests within 1-3 business days. If approved, funds will be returned to your original payment method within 5-10 business days."

=== Disputes & Chargebacks ===
Resolve with support first. "Fraudulent or unjustified chargebacks may result in account suspension."
