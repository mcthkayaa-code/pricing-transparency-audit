Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 7, 2024

1.Introduction

Welcome to Shorts Faceless. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you how we handle your personal data when you visit our website https://shortsfaceless.com (hereafter "Website") and inform you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. Data We Collect About You

We collect and process personal data about you when you interact with us and our Website. This includes:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Payment information
  • YouTube account refresh token (when you connect your YouTube account)
  • Links to generated videos (stored for 7 days)
  • User-generated content (text, images, audio) for AI processing
  • Usage data and analytics

We also collect non-personal data such as web cookies and referral tokens when applicable.

Links to privacy policies of other third-party service providers used by the app:

  • TikTok Terms of Service

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Process your orders
  • Allow you to connect your YouTube account
  • Display the videos you generated on your dashboard
  • Generate AI-powered content based on your inputs
  • Analyze and improve our services

4. Sharing Your Personal Data and Use of AI Services

We use various AI services and analytics tools to provide and improve our services. This includes:

AI Models:

  • Text generation: OpenAI models, Anthropic Claude models, Google models
  • Image generation: Stability SDXL (Amazon Bedrock), Replicate text-to-image models
  • Text-to-speech: ElevenLabs models, OpenAI models, Google models
  • Speech-to-text: Google models, OpenAI models

Analytics:

  • PostHog
  • Plausible

When you use our services, your inputs (such as text prompts, images, or audio) may be processed by these AI models to generate the desired output. We do not share your personal information with these AI providers beyond what is necessary to process your requests.

For analytics purposes, we use PostHog and Plausible to collect usage data. This helps us understand how our services are used and how we can improve them.

5. User Consent for AI Processing and Data Sharing

By using our services, you consent to the processing of your inputs by our AI models and the collection of usage data by our analytics tools. We obtain explicit consent for these processes in the following ways:

  1. When you sign up for our service, we present our terms of service and this privacy policy, which you must agree to before creating an account.
  2. Before processing any user-generated content with AI models, we present a clear notification explaining that the content will be processed by AI.
  3. We provide options in your account settings to opt-out of non-essential data collection and AI processing.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us or deleting your account.
