REFUND (refund_policy_exists, refund_window_days, refund_conditions):
"Fees and Payments... All fees are in U.S. Dollars and are non-refundable, and Sapling
will not prorate any fees paid for a subscription that is terminated before the end of
its term."

AUTO-RENEWAL (auto_renewal_default):
"Subscription Service. The Service may include automatically recurring payments for
periodic charges ("Subscription Service"). If you activate a Subscription Service, you
authorize Sapling to periodically charge, on a going-forward basis and until
cancellation of either the recurring payments or your account, all accrued sums... Your
account will be charged automatically on the Subscription Billing Date all applicable
fees for the next subscription period. The subscription will continue unless and until
you cancel your subscription or we terminate it. You must cancel your subscription
before it renews in order to avoid billing of the next periodic subscription fee to
your account."

CANCELLATION ROUTE (cancellation_self_serve):
"You may cancel the Subscription Service by contacting us at: support@sapling.ai"
-- No self-serve account-settings cancellation path is mentioned anywhere in the ToS.
-- CONFLICTS with billing_faq (see billing-faq-full.txt), which tells users to cancel
   via a self-serve "Manage billing" dashboard. ToS ranks first for contractual
   variables per protocol/codebook section 6.2 / section 3, so cancellation_self_serve
   is coded from the ToS ("contact_required"), and the disagreement is logged in
   conflict_note per codebook section 2.1 step 3 / protocol section 6.2.

RENEWAL NOTICE (renewal_notice_commitment): no clause found anywhere in the document
committing to notify the buyer in advance of a renewal charge. The only "notice" clause
found concerns changes to the Terms themselves ("Changes... effective upon the earlier
of thirty (30) calendar days following our dispatch of an e-mail notice... or thirty (30)
calendar days following our posting of notice"), which is not a renewal-billing notice.

OWNERSHIP / IP (output_ownership_statement, commercial_use_lowest_tier,
watermark_removal_tier): the ONLY ownership/IP clauses in the document are:
1) "Ownership. Excluding any User Content that you may provide, you acknowledge that
   all the intellectual property rights... in the Service and its content are owned by
   Sapling or Sapling's suppliers." (about the SERVICE/software itself, not about
   detection results generated for the user)
2) "User Content" is defined as "any and all information and content that a user
   submits to, or uses with, the Service (e.g., text or other content you provide that
   is analyzed by the Service)" -- i.e. the INPUT text the user pastes in, not the
   detector's output/score.
3) User Content License: "You hereby grant... to Sapling an irrevocable, nonexclusive,
   royalty-free and fully paid, worldwide license to reproduce, distribute, prepare
   derivative works of... your User Content... solely for the purposes of providing the
   Service..." -- again about the user's INPUT, not the score/report the tool returns.
No clause anywhere addresses ownership of, or a commercial-use license for, the
detection score / per-sentence report / share certificate the AI Detector generates.
No watermark clause of any kind appears in the document.
Combined with the ai-content-detector page's own description of its output as a
probability score (see ai-content-detector-page-excerpts.txt), this supports coding
commercial_use_lowest_tier, watermark_removal_tier and output_ownership_statement as
not_applicable (no output these constructs could attach to) rather than unknown (which
would be the code if the product generated an artifact and the documents were merely
silent about it -- codebook section 2.2's own distinction).

ACCEPTABLE USE / DISCRETIONARY QUERY LIMIT (usage_cap_quantified,
unquantified_limit_clause):
"Acceptable Use Policy... you agree not to: (i) submit or generate more than a
reasonable number of searches, requests, or queries, determined in Sapling's sole
discretion, and in no event more than the number allowed under your subscribed level of
Service..."
-- states a volume ceiling with NO number attached ("reasonable number... sole
discretion"), tied explicitly to "your subscribed level of Service" (i.e. it applies at
every tier, including the entry paid tier, Pro, on top of the tier's quantified
100,000-character-per-check cap documented on the ai-content-detector page).
