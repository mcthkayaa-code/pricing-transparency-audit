BILLING FAQ (trial_auto_converts, cancellation_self_serve):
Q: "Why was I automatically started on a subscription when signing up?"
A: "While you may have been started on a free trial, you were not subscribed. You are
only subscribed when you provide payment information at https://sapling.ai/plans. The
free trial automatically cancels if no payment method is added."
-- Read as: the trial does NOT auto-convert into a paid subscription; by default (no
card added) it lapses/cancels on its own. Becoming a paying subscriber requires the
user's own action of adding payment info, not passive/automatic conversion.

Q: "Where can I update my payment information or cancel my plan?"
A: "Please sign in and visit https://sapling.ai/dashboard-billing and click Manage
billing. There you can manage your billing information and subscription. Please note
that you must have subscribed using the same email address you used to sign up for
Sapling."
-- This describes a SELF-SERVE cancellation route (a billing dashboard), which conflicts
with the Terms of Service's "cancel by contacting support@sapling.ai" (see
tos-key-clauses.txt). See conflict_note in the record; coded from the ToS per the
protocol's source-authority hierarchy for contractual variables.
