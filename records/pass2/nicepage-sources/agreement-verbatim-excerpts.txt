--- Miscellaneous, item 1 (refund) ---
"The Nicepage.com website is under no obligation to refund your subscription fee under any
circumstances. In case Nicepage.com determines that you are entitled to a refund of all or part of
your subscription fee, such refund shall only be made to the credit card account originally used
by you to purchase your Subscription. If your Subscription was paid by check, your refund would be
made by check."

--- Miscellaneous, item 2 (use of output / non-transferability) ---
""Non-transferable" as used herein means that except as specifically provided in this Agreement,
you may not sell, rent, loan, give, sub-license, or otherwise transfer to anyone the Work or the
right to use the Work. However, the Work you produce with the Work must be used for yourself, your
direct employer, client, or customer, who must be the end-user of your Work."
(Read as: the templates/assets ("Work") you download are non-transferable, but what YOU BUILD with
them may be used for yourself / employer / client / customer -- i.e. commercial delivery to a
client is contemplated and not restricted to any specific paid tier. No ownership statement for
the buyer's own created website/output is made anywhere in this document -- this clause addresses
USE, not OWNERSHIP.)

--- Miscellaneous, item 3 (seat/device restriction) ---
"You may use your Subscription on ONE COMPUTER at any one time. If any two users on two separate
computers use the same username and password, we reserve the right to terminate that subscriber's
account without a refund or prior notice."
(A concurrent-device restriction, itself already quantified ("one computer"); not treated as an
unquantified fair-use/throttling clause.)

--- Bold/caps block near the end (breach termination) ---
"NICEPAGE.COM SHALL BE UNDER NO OBLIGATION TO REFUND ANY FEES PAID BY YOU IN THE EVENT THAT YOUR
ACCOUNT IS TERMINATED BY REASON OF A BREACH ANY SUCH BREACH OR BREACHES."
(Scoped specifically to termination-for-breach, not the general refund case.)

No statement anywhere in this document about: auto-renewal default state, self-serve cancellation
route, or an explicit ownership statement for the buyer's own generated website.

No "fair use" / "reasonable use" / "excessive use" / "abuse threshold" / discretionary-throttling
clause tied to usage VOLUME found anywhere in the full document (read to the end). Restrictions
list (13 items) is entirely about conduct/redistribution/misuse of downloaded template assets, not
about volume or intensity of product use.
