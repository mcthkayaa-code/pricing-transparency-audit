1) https://nicepage.com/doc/16656/faq ("Frequently Asked Questions")
   Accessed: 2026-08-15
   Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260815122651/https://nicepage.com/doc/16656/faq

   "Do you give a trial period to test premium features?
   After creating a Nicepage account, you start the 7-day Trial Period, which allows you to test
   most Premium Features. Some features remain available only for Premium Plans."

   "What is the difference between a subscription and a license?
   Subscription: It is a one-year Premium for the Nicepage Desktop Applications, Online Builders,
   WordPress Plugin, and Joomla Extension, including one year of updates. Auto-renewal is
   optional; you can buy a Subscription without it. The auto-renewal price has a 40% discount for
   the following year's purchase.
   License: It is a lifetime Premium for the Desktop Application, WordPress Plugin, and Joomla
   Extension. It also includes one year of updates. If you refuse to pay for the updates in the
   next year, you can continue to use your current version or any other prior release for a
   lifetime."

   "Can I cancel my Premium Plan at any time?
   There is no need to cancel a License or Subscription without auto-renewal, which expires
   automatically. If you have a Subscription with auto-renewal enabled, you can withdraw it at any
   time in your Profile or during purchase.
   If you need a refund, please refer to the Terms to determine your eligibility, then contact our
   support with your request."

   "Can I buy the Subscription without Auto-Renewal?
   Yes. You can do that on the Payment Page. For more information, please visit here."

   No statement anywhere on this page about a credit card being required to start the trial.

   Accessed: 2026-08-15

   "To use Nicepage, you must register on the Nicepage website or in the Windows or Mac OS
   applications. The Trial Period starts right after the registration, and you can use the

      registration flow is barred by the codebook).

3) https://nicepage.com/doc/548073/how-to-cancel-the-auto-renewal ("How to Cancel Auto-Renewal")
   Accessed: 2026-08-15
   Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260815122735/https://nicepage.com/doc/548073/how-to-cancel-the-auto-renewal

   "Buy Without Auto-Renewal
   You can buy a Subscription without Auto-Renewal. Clicking the Buy button, you get to the
   Checkout page. On the Checkout page, you click the Auto-renewal link. After clicking the link,
   you will see the dialog on which you can click the Switch to Manual Renewal link.

   Cancel Auto-Renewal At Any Time
   You can cancel the auto-renewal of your Premium Plan at any time by following the steps.
   Open the Licences and Subscription page from your user Profile.
   Click the Unsubscribe link to cancel Auto-renewal."
   => Checkout defaults to Automatic Renewal; buyer must actively "Switch to Manual Renewal" to
      opt out. auto_renewal_default = on. Cancellation is self-serve from the Profile ("Licences
      and Subscription" page, "Unsubscribe" link). cancellation_self_serve = self_serve_documented.
      No advance-notice-before-charge commitment stated anywhere in this article.
      renewal_notice_commitment = unknown.
