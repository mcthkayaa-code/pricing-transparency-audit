--- FAQ accordion (all expanded manually, official page content) ---

Q: How much does Adobe Firefly cost?
A: Adobe Firefly offers a free plan with limited daily generations, plus four paid tiers:
   Adobe Firefly Standard: $9.99/month — 2,000 generative credits
   Adobe Firefly Pro: $19.99/month — 4,000 generative credits
   Adobe Firefly Pro Plus: $49.99/month — 10,000 generative credits
   Adobe Firefly Premium: $199.99/month — 50,000 generative credits plus unlimited Adobe Firefly Video Model access
   All paid plans include unlimited standard image and vector generations. Generative credits are used for
   premium features such as video, audio, and partner model outputs.

Q: Is there a free version of Adobe Firefly?
A: Yes, Adobe has a free plan where users receive free daily generations to try Adobe Firefly. If you need
   more than what's included in your plan, you will need a paid subscription to continue generating.

Q: What are generative credits and how do they work?
A: Generative credits are tokens you use to generate AI content across Adobe Firefly and Adobe Creative
   Cloud apps. Adobe Firefly plans include a monthly allocation of generative credits that reset each
   billing cycle and do not roll over. [link: Learn more about generative credits]

Q: What happens when I run out of generative credits on my Adobe Firefly plan?
A: When you run out of generative credits, you can wait for your monthly reset, upgrade to a higher Adobe
   Firefly plan, or apply a credit add-on plan to your existing subscription starting at 2,000 credits. On
   any paid Adobe Firefly plan, you still have unlimited access to standard generative features even after
   your generative credits are exhausted. Adobe will not charge you for additional credits unless you
   choose to purchase them.

Q: Are Adobe Firefly outputs safe for commercial use?
A: Outputs from Adobe Firefly AI models are safe for commercial use. Adobe Firefly models are trained on
   licensed content and public domain content where copyright has expired, and Adobe automatically attaches
   Content Credentials for transparency. You're responsible for determining whether content generated by
   partner models is appropriate for your project. Eligible enterprise customers can also receive IP
   indemnification for select Adobe Firefly outputs. [link: Learn more about Adobe Firefly for enterprise]

Q: What does "commercial use" mean?
A: Commercial use refers to any type of usage that generates commercial activity for you, your brand, or
   your business — for example, selling products, creating print and digital promotional materials, or
   using content in the sale of goods and services. Outputs generated with Adobe Firefly AI models can be
   used for commercial purposes. Adobe Firefly models are trained on licensed content and public domain
   content, giving creators confidence when using outputs in commercial projects.
