HumanizeMyAI is a digital subscription service operated by Mihci AI Studios LLC. This policy
explains when access begins, how to cancel a subscription, and how refund and billing-dispute
requests are handled.

Digital delivery
Paid features become available after payment is confirmed. There is no physical product, shipping,
or delivery charge.

Free evaluation
HumanizeMyAI offers a free tier that does not require a payment card. Customers can evaluate the
core service before purchasing a subscription.

Cancellation
- You may cancel a subscription at any time from your account.
- Cancellation stops future renewals.
- Access continues through the end of the already-paid billing period.
- If self-service cancellation is unavailable, email hello@humanizemy.ai and we will process the
  request within one business day.

Refund eligibility
Paid subscription charges are generally non-refundable after digital access is provided. We will
issue or consider a refund when required by applicable law, when a duplicate or incorrect charge
occurred, or when a documented material service failure prevented access to the paid service.

How to request a refund or raise a billing dispute
- Email hello@humanizemy.ai from the address associated with your account.
- Include the charge date, amount, and a short explanation. Do not send full card details.
- We will review the request and respond within 14 days.
- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Bank and card-network processing
  times may apply.

Contact

+1 (307) 218-6489

See also the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy.
