=== SERIES & VIDEOS (relevant items) ===

Q: How many videos can I create per day?
A: The number of videos created by each series can be seen on our pricing page. Remember, AutoShorts
doesn't focus on individual video creation. Instead, you set up a Series that automatically generates
videos on a schedule. Features that render a new video such as making script edits, or changing your
series do not count against your plan.

Q: Can I adjust the video length?
A: When creating your series, you can choose between "30 to 60 seconds" or "60 to 90 seconds" length
options. For more fine-tuned length control you can manually modify the length of your AI generated
script, up to 1,600 characters max.

Q: Will I get banned?
A: Our service has been reviewed and approved by TikTok and YouTube. However, we recommend that brand
new channels do not post more than once per day to avoid being flagged as spam; you should "warm" up
these accounts. You should also preview your videos before posting to ensure that they are appropriate
for your audience.
[Note: this is guidance about third-party platform spam policies, not a self-imposed discretionary usage
throttle by AutoShorts itself.]

Q: Do I own the videos?
A: Yes, the videos are yours to do with as you please. You can download them and use them on other
platforms, or even sell them to clients.

Q: What are image credits?
A: By default, our AI models generate images for your videos. However, sometimes the images are not
perfect, and you may need to generate a replacement image. Image credits allow you to generate new
ones. Your plan includes a certain number of image credits that refill on a 30 day rolling basis, but
you can purchase more if you need them.
[Note: no per-tier image-credit COUNT is published anywhere on the pricing grid; only Motion Credits
counts (27/62/124) are shown per tier.]

Q: What are motion credits?
A: By default, our AI creates still images for each scene in your video. Motion credits can animate
those images. When setting up your series (or in your series settings), you can specify how many
motion credits you are okay using per video, and our AI will automatically use them in your video. You
can also manually use them in the media editor to add motion to still images.
Each plan comes with motion credits that reset each billing cycle, but you can also purchase "top off"
credits that never expire from the billing tab. Feel free to setup auto-reload if you'd like to
automate top offs.

Q: Why does the background music stop at 60 seconds?
A: Unfortunately, TikTok does not currently allow uploaded music to be longer than 1 minute. Otherwise,
you risk your video being muted or taken down. Once TikTok allows this, so will we!

Q: How long will I have access to my videos?
A: You can download past videos in your series for up to 120 days, after which they are removed from
our servers. If you delete your series, all associated videos are also deleted, so be sure to download
any you want to keep.

=== BILLING (all items) ===

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: You bet your sweet bippy there is. Simply create an account and you can create your first series for
free to test a video. No credit card required.
Update: We have temporarily suspended the free plan due to abuse from botting / spamming. We will be
re-enabling it soon once we implement a better bot detection system.

Q: Can I cancel at anytime?
A: Absolutely. We hate services that purposefully make it difficult to cancel. You can cancel at the
click of a button from the dashboard's billing page.

Q: How does the membership work?
A: Beyond the free plan, we offer different tiers of paid memberships. The paid plans remove the
watermark and allow you to post more frequently.

Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Unfortunately, we cannot offer refunds as costs incurred for creating AI videos and generating AI
photos are extremely high. In turn, our upstream providers do not let us ask for refunds for the GPU
processing time used to create your AI videos. This would make it a loss making endeavor for us.
During sign up you agree to withhold your right to refund for this reason. You can cancel any time
though and your subscription ends.

Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription?
A: Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your subscription at any time. Go to the billing tab and select
the plan you want to upgrade / downgrade to. If you move to a plan with less series than you currently
have, the extra series will be automatically disabled.

Q: Can I have multiple plans?
A: Unfortunately, no. An AutoShorts account can only be on one plan "type" at a time. For example, you
can't have one series on the Daily plan and another on the Hardcore plan; they would both need to be on
the same plan type. The workaround is to create a separate account if you require multiple plans.
