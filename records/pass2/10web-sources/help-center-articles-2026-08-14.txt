ARTICLE 1
URL: https://help.10web.io/hc/en-us/articles/22592225897490-AI-Starter-Plan
TITLE: AI Starter Plan
CATEGORY PATH: Getting Started > Business Plans
================================================================================
Best for

The AI Starter plan is designed for individuals, small businesses, startups, and website owners
who need one professional WordPress website with AI-powered website creation, managed hosting,
speed optimization, and essential website management tools.

This plan is a good fit if you want to create and manage a single website with 10Web's Agentic
Website Builder, use 10Web Premium Hosting, and access the main tools needed to launch, edit,
optimize, and maintain your site.

What's included

1. High-performance WordPress hosting
Managed WordPress hosting: Fast and reliable 10Web Premium Hosting for 1 website.
99.99% uptime.
Monthly visitors: The plan supports up to 10,000 visitors per month. For every 10K extra
visitors, you'll be charged an extra $2 at the end of the billing month. Visits are measured by
the total monthly traffic, based on unique IPs, to your site.
SSD storage: Includes 10GB scalable storage with fees for overages if you exceed the limit.
You'll be charged an extra $2 at the end of the billing month for each extra 5GB used.
Free SSL certificate.
Free custom domain: The AI Starter plan includes a free custom domain for 1 year, up to $30.
Bot and DDoS protection.
Access to website files and database (SSH, SFTP, MySQL).
Hosting usage analytics.
Staging test environment.
Automated 1-click migration.
Multisite support.

2. Agentic Website Builder
Agentic Website Builder: Create a production-ready WordPress website with AI agents.
AI credits: The AI Starter plan includes 100 AI credits per month. These credits are used for
AI-powered website generation and editing actions available in the platform.
Prompt-to-website generation.
Redesign agent, Cloning agent, Figma agent.
Advanced AI Editor.
Mobile optimized, custom domain integration, Full WordPress CMS.

3. Ecommerce dashboard — product/order management, 10Web Payments, analytics, checkout, tax
setup, product filter, customer accounts.

4. Website speed optimization — full optimization, 90+ PageSpeed score, Core Web Vitals, SEO
foundations, image optimization, full page caching, dynamic ecommerce caching.

5. 24/7 customer support — email + chat.

6. Collaboration and automated website maintenance — user/team management, automated WP/plugin
updates, automated daily backups, activity log, up-to-date PHP/MySQL.

What's not included
Cloudflare CDN: Access to Cloudflare Enterprise CDN requires an additional purchase (Website
Booster tab > Cloudflare CDN > Buy Now).
White Label for WordPress: Available only with Agency plans.
VIP priority support: Available only with Agency plans.

Price and billing cycle
"For pricing details about the AI Starter plan, please visit this page [pricing page]. You have
the option to choose between monthly, annual, and longer-term billing cycles when available."
"Longer billing cycles may be more cost-effective than monthly billing. Please check the pricing
page or your checkout screen for the current billing options and savings."

Upgrade options
Adding more websites: additional-website pricing may vary by billing cycle, shown at checkout.
Upgrading to a higher plan: via Subscription Plan page > Upgrade.

Upgrade limitations
"You will have limitations if: Your current plan, including add-ons such as additional websites
and CDN, has a higher monthly or annual price than the plan you want to upgrade to. Your current
plan's storage and visitor limits, including add-ons, are higher than the plan you want to
upgrade to." Contact support for assistance in these cases.

Downgrading to a lower plan
"Downgrading is a manual process handled by our Billing Team, as it cannot be performed directly
through your account." Requires contacting Customer Care.

Common charges — Subscription renewal
"10Web charges subscription plan fees automatically upon renewal. There are two models: Monthly
subscription: Charged monthly on the same date as your initial payment. Annual subscription: Paid
upfront for a year and automatically renewed annually on the same date."
"You can switch to an annual or monthly payment schedule at any time by contacting customer
support."

Free trial expiration
"We provide a 7-day free trial to help you evaluate our services without any charges. You can
freely cancel your subscription during this trial period from your Subscription Plan tab in your
10Web Workspace."
"**The 7-day free trial automatically upgrades to a paid subscription unless cancelled within the
trial period.**"
"Modifying any paid plan features/inclusions during your free trial (e.g. disabling 'Site Lock'
and/or obtaining a domain, Websites, CDN, etc), ends the trial immediately, upgrading to a paid
plan and charging your payment method."
"Important: 7-day trial is only available for platform plans. Speed Booster offers a freemium
version, requiring immediate payment for upgrades."

Add-ons
"Additional features, such as extra hosted site slots or Cloudflare Enterprise CDNs, are billed
as add-ons to your plan. Review these in your Workspace Subscription Plan tab. To remove add-ons
you purchased earlier, contact Customer Care."

Plan resource overages
"Charges may apply for exceeding resource limits (e.g., visitors, CDN bandwidth, storage)
included in your plan. For more details, refer to the dedicated overages article."

Timeframe of the charges
"Credit card payments: Processed at 12:00 UTC. PayPal payments: Processed between 10:00 and 14:00
UTC. Time zone differences might cause a one-day discrepancy in your statement."
