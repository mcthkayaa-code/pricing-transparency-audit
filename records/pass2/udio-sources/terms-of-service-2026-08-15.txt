Interpretive tension flagged for coder_note: Section 5.2 flatly bars commercial exploitation of Output (no tier carve-out, "written permission" only as a generic savings clause). Section 6.3 grants use "solely for... personal and non-commercial purposes" (no download at all). Section 6.4 waives only the ATTRIBUTION requirement for paid subscribers making public use of Output -- it does not contain the words "commercial" and does not affirmatively state paid subscribers may monetize Output. Read together, the document's only explicit, on-point statements about commercial use are prohibitive, so commercial_use_lowest_tier is coded not_granted from 5.2 + 6.3, with the 6.4 tension disclosed rather than silently resolved.
