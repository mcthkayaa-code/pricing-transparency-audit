=== https://help.udio.com/en/articles/10739134-credits-and-credit-limits ===
Title: Credits and credit limits | Udio Help Center
Dated on page: October 29, 2025
Accessed: 2026-08-15

Udio credits enable you to create songs on Udio.

Spending credits
Every time you click "Create", "Extend", "Remix", "Inpaint" or "Edit," two new songs are created.
Trimming a song does not cost any credits.
Each set of two 32-second songs costs two credits (one credit for each song)
Each set of two 130-second songs costs four credits (two credits for each song)
NOTE: When on a free account, you can only create three 130-second songs per day, regardless of how many credits you have.

Receiving credits
Free accounts: 10 daily credits; when exhausted, pull from an additional monthly limit of 100 credits. These are credit limits; credits do not roll over. At the start of your second month, you'll again have a 10-daily limit and 100-monthly limit.
Accounts with subscriptions ("Paid accounts"): Standard subscription = up to 2400 credits per month. Pro subscription = up to 6000 credits per month.

Important notes
"Subscriptions come with credit limits. The credits don't rollover from month to month, and if your subscription ends (due to cancelation or failed payment), your account will revert to the free-account credit limits."
"Our trials have two key limitations until/unless converted into a subscription: Trials do not come with increased credit limits; accounts on subscription trials still have the free-tier credit limits. Subscription trials do not include unlimited 'u-130' (~2 minute song) creations, but -- like free-tier accounts -- are capped at creating 3 u-130 songs per day."
"You may convert a trial immediately to a paid subscription at any time at udio.com/pricing"
"All accounts: Credits are available for purchase a la carte at udio.com/pricing. These credits never expire."

=== https://help.udio.com/en/articles/13338649-the-subscription-trial ===
Title: The subscription trial | Udio Help Center
Dated on page: January 10, 2026
Accessed: 2026-08-15

"All creators have the one-time option to initiate a free trial of Udio's Standard subscription."
"Trials last for up to 7 days from the moment initiated."
"Trials are cancelable at udio.com/account."
"If not cancelled before the end of the 7 day period, the creator will be immediately charged the full price of an annual subscription unless they previously toggled the subscription period to monthly."
"Each creator may initiate only one subscription trial total."
"The subscription trial is for a subset of the Standard subscription features; we do not currently offer a Pro subscription trial."
During trial, activated: up to 6 simultaneous songs, Voice Control, music/lyrics editing, audio-upload-guided generations, use existing songs as style reference, custom cover art upload.
During trial, NOT available: increased credit limit; more than three 2-minute songs/day.
Example: trial initiated Feb 10 2026; upgraded Feb 14; "immediately charged the full price for an annual subscription (e.g., $96 + tax in the U.S.)"; "Their subscription will be renewed on Feb 10, 2027 and their card charged the then-listed-price for an annual subscription."
Edge case: failed/pending payment -> account reverts to free tier after a few days / within one week if not approved.
No statement anywhere in this article that a credit card must be provided to START the trial -- only that a card ON FILE will be CHARGED at conversion. No "no card required" statement either.
