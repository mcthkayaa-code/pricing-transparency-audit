When does the daily limit refresh?
With a free plan, you have a limit of 10 credits per day. These credits refresh 24 hours after creating your first song (e.g., if you create a song at 10 AM, you have 10 credits until 10 AM the next day).

How does the free plan monthly limit work?
Once you've used up the daily credits, you begin to use up monthly limit. The monthly limit cycle starts from first song created or previous subscription end and refreshes on the same date of the following month.

How do the paid tier credit systems work?
The credits refresh a month after your payment goes through and the plan starts. The paid plans do not include the 10 daily credit limits or the 100 monthly credit limits.

How does the free trial work?
During the free trial, you can access all Standard features but remain on the free tier credit limits (i.e. 10/day). The free trial can be converted to a paid plan at any time from the pricing page.

What is a credit?
1 credit corresponds to 1 ~30 second audio track. Each generation generates 2 tracks, so each generation costs 2 credits. The udio130 2-min model will be correspondingly higher

What else are credits used for?
Credits are only used for generating new tracks currently.

Can purchased credits expire?
No, purchased credits never expire, however monthly credits granted as part of your plan (e.g. 1200 credits for standard) will reset one month after your plan start date.

What happens if I run out of credits?
If you run out of credits, you will be unable to generate new tracks or cover art until more credits are purchased. You can buy additional credits anytime from this page at https://www.udio.com/pricing.

What features are included in the free plan?
You are able to Remix and Extend tracks in the free plan but some features will only be available to the paid plans. You will have a limited number of udio130 2-min generations as well.
