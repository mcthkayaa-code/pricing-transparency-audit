The problem

Great videos still die on a cold account

You can post the best short-form in your category and watch it reach 84 people, because the account carrying it has no history the algorithm can read. The apps and ecom brands all over your For You page solved this before they solved their content: they run fleets of warmed accounts, and every video gets more than one chance to hit.

Day 1

New accounts sit in a cold-start pool

No watch history, no device age, no engagement graph. There's nothing to classify you with, so the platform spends almost no impressions finding out.

Emulator

The device is the first thing checked

Emulators, cloud phones and Android farms are trivially detectable. If the handset doesn't look like a real person's phone, nothing above it matters.

Unknown

Bought accounts carry someone else's history

A marketplace account arrives with prior device fingerprints, IP records and whatever it was trained to be about. You inherit all of it, and you find out after you've posted.

Why humans

Automation leaves fingerprints

Emulators, cloud phones, posting APIs, warming scripts. Every machine shortcut leaves a trace the platforms are built to find, and the detection gets better every quarter. So we run the one setup that has nothing to detect: a person, on a real iPhone, for every single account.

Day-to-day

You queue it. A person posts it.

Videos wait in your queue until an operator picks them up and publishes from the device, IP and behavioural pattern the account was built on. No script ever touches your accounts. That continuity is the whole reason the reach holds, and it is why we don't hand out logins or go near the publishing API.

You queue

Push a video from the Faceless dashboard or through the API, and pick which handles should run it.

We review

A quick check for anything that would put an account at risk, plus caption, sound and hashtag selection per platform.

A human posts

An operator picks your video off the queue and posts it from the account's own device, on its own IP, at a slot that suits its audience. Never the publishing API.

You see results

Views, follows and engagement flow back per account, so you can tell which handles are carrying your distribution.

Why this costs us more, on purpose. Content published through the official APIs is flagged as API-published and generally gets more conservative distribution than the same video posted from the app. Paying people to post by hand is the expensive option. It is also the reason these accounts keep reaching.

Trust & safety

What we will and won't do

Built to last, covered when it doesn't

Purchased followers are inert and detectable, and they wreck the engagement ratio the algorithm scores you on. Every follower on your accounts arrived because a real person did something real.

Replacement, not excuses

If an account is actioned for anything other than the content you supplied, we replace it with another warmed account at no cost. Content-driven bans fall outside that, which is why we review what goes into the queue.

One brand per account

Your accounts serve your brand and nobody else's. We don't recycle handles between customers and we don't run competing accounts on your material.

Honest about the risk

Every platform can action any account at any time. Anyone promising you that will never happen is selling you something. We build for durability and cover you when it doesn't hold.
