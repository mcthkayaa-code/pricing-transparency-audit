Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Faceless AI video generation

How does Faceless AI video generation work?
Faceless uses advanced AI to generate engaging videos from your prompts, Reddit posts, or blogs. Simply input your content, select a template, and our AI handles the rest - creating professional videos with captions, B-rolls, and more.
Can I customize the videos?
Absolutely! You can customize every aspect of your videos including templates, fonts, colors, transitions, B-rolls, and more. Our platform offers extensive customization options to match your brand and style.
How many videos can I create per day?
You can create as many videos per day as you have credits for. Each video generation costs approximately 20 credits. Your available credits depend on your subscription plan, and you can always purchase additional credits if needed.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. There are no long-term commitments.
How do I view how many credits I have left?
You can view your usage on the billing page by clicking your profile picture in the top right corner.
Do you offer a free trial?
We don't offer a traditional free trial at the moment, but we have competitive pricing and a satisfaction guarantee.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will be prorated based on your billing cycle.
Can I monetize videos created with Faceless?
Yes. You fully own the rights to all videos. We use custom recorded gameplay to ensure originality.
Can I import my own content into Faceless?
Yes. You can import your own content during the AI workflow or while in the editor.
