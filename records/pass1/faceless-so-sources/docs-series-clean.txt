Creating a series is free; each generated episode charges the credit cost of its model (20 to 100 credits), exactly like a single video. Create a series (POST /series, scope series:write) Required: name , source , and a niche or customPrompt . Everything else is optional and gets product defaults. Enumerate valid values first: GET /options?kind=sources , ?kind=niches , ?kind=styles , ?kind=languages , ?kind=backgrounds , ?kind=music , ?kind=captionThemes , and GET /voices . bash curl -s -X POST https://faceless.so/api/v1/series \ -H &quot;Authorization: Bearer $FACELESS_API_KEY&quot; \ -H &quot;Content-Type: application/json&quot; \ -H &quot;Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)&quot; \ -d &#039;{ &quot;name&quot;: &quot;Deep sea facts&quot;, &quot;source&quot;: &quot;Facts &amp; stories&quot;, &quot;niche&quot;: &quot;Ocean facts&quot;, &quot;voice&quot;: &quot;EXAVITQu4vr4xnSDxMaL&quot;, &quot;duration&quot;: &quot;60&quot;, &quot;autoPostTime&quot;: &quot;18:00&quot;, &quot;timezone&quot;: &quot;America/New_York&quot; }&#039; The first episode starts generating right after creation. Useful optional fields: destination and destinationAccounts : auto-post platform and connected account ids (from GET /accounts ) postingDays : e.g. [&quot;Monday&quot;, &quot;Wednesday&quot;, &quot;Friday&quot;] ; omit for every day brollModel : storyboard , motion_lite or motion_pro , which sets the per-episode credit cost subreddit : when source is &quot;Reddit post&quot; backgroundVideo or useRandomBackgroundVideo : gameplay/footage behind the captions captionStyle , showEmojis , enableBackgroundMusic , backgroundMusicMood , hashtags , tone , youtubePrivacyStatus Rate limit: 5 series creations per 300s per key. Track episodes (GET /series/{id}/episodes, scope series:read) Episodes are regular videos plus scheduling state. Newest first, paginated: bash curl -s &quot;https://faceless.so/api/v1/series/665f1b2a9c31a2b3c4d5e901/episodes?limit=10&quot; \ -H &quot;Authorization: Bearer $FACELESS_API_KEY&quot; json { &quot;success&quot;: true, &quot;data&quot;: [{ &quot;id&quot;: &quot;665f1b2a9c31a2b3c4d5eb01&quot;, &quot;projectId&quot;: &quot;665f1b2a9c31a2b3c4d5ea01&quot;, &quot;status&quot;: &quot;posted&quot;, &quot;scheduledTime&quot;: &quot;2026-07-30T18:00:00.000Z&quot; }], &quot;pagination&quot;: { &quot;page&quot;: 1, &quot;limit&quot;: 10, &quot;total&quot;: 1 } } Poll an episode's generation with GET /videos/{projectId}/status like any other video. Force the next episode now (POST /series/{id}/generate, scope series:write) Generates the next episode immediately instead of waiting for the schedule. Charges the series' per-episode cost. Returns the new projectId and its statusUrl . Rate limit: 5 per 300s. bash curl -s -X POST https://faceless.so/api/v1/series/665f1b2a9c31a2b3c4d5e901/generate \ -H &quot;Authorization: Bearer $FACELESS_API_KEY&quot; \ -H &quot;Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)&quot; Update, pause, delete GET /series and GET /series/{id} (scope series:read): list and inspect configuration. PATCH /series/{id} (scope series:write): change any subset of fields; only the fields you send change. Pass {&quot;paused&quot;: true} to pause automatic generation, false to resume. DELETE /series/{id} (scope series:write): stops all future generation; already-generated videos are kept. bash curl -s -X PATCH https://faceless.so/api/v1/series/665f1b2a9c31a2b3c4d5e901 \ -H &quot;Authorization: Bearer $FACELESS_API_KEY&quot; \ -H &quot;Content-Type: application/json&quot; \ -d &#039;{&quot;autoPostTime&quot;: &quot;09:00&quot;, &quot;paused&quot;: false}&#039; Budgeting An everyday series on storyboard spends 20 credits per episode: a daily series is about 600 credits per month, a Monday/Wednesday/Friday series about 260. Check the balance with GET /credits before creating aggressive schedules; an episode that cannot be paid for fails with insufficient_credits . See credits and limits .
