Voice Generation Time (VGT)
Validity
Voice generation time is carried forward when your plan auto-renews or is paused.
Active and paused plan VGT is carried forward to the next billing cycle.
Monthly plan
If you have a monthly plan set to auto-renew, any remaining VGT at the end of the month will carry forward to the next month. The carried over VGT will be added as additional VGT for the next cycle, while the VGT gained for the new subscription is referred to as Plan VGT.
For example, if you purchased a Creator monthly plan on August 3rd and used 1 hour of VGT, your account will receive 2 hours of plan VGT when it auto-renews on September 3rd. The remaining 1 hour of VGT from August to September will also be added as additional VGT.
The carried over VGT from the last billing cycle will only be carried forward to the following month.
If carried over VGT from August is unused in September, it will not carry over to October.
If a subscription is cancelled, the carried over VGT will be lost . However, if the subscription is paused, the carried over VGT will remain.
Yearly plans
The same principle applies to yearly subscriptions, where any unused VGT at the end of the subscription period is carried forward to the next annual subscription.
For instance, suppose you purchased a Creator Yearly plan in January 2026 and utilized 12 hours of VGT out of the allotted 24 hours. When your plan automatically renews in January 2027, your account will receive 24 hours of plan VGT for the new cycle spanning from January 2027 to January 2028, and the remaining 12 hours of VGT from January 2026 to January 2027 will be added as additional VGT.
The carried over VGT from the previous billing cycle will only be carried forward to the next year.
If unused VGT from the cycle of January 26' - 27' will not carry over to the January 28' - 29' cycle.
If a subscription is canceled, the carried over VGT will be lost . However, if the subscription is paused, the carried over VGT will remain.
