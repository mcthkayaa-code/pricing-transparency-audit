Subscription Auto-renewal
Murf plans are designed to make your life easier. With automatic renewals, you don't have to worry about manually renewing your plan every month or year.
If you don't wish to renew your subscription, you can cancel or pause your plan before your last paid period ends. You can check the   Billing & Payments tab in Workspace Settings for the date.
Only Admins/Owners can Manage Payments for Enterprise and leagcy team workspaces.
