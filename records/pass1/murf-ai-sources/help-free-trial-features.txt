Free Trial Features
Murf Free Trial lets you explore and test out all voices and premium features of the Studio. All first-time users/signups are automatically assigned a Free Trial.
Features of the free trial:
Access all  300+ voices across 33 languages
No downloads included
10 minutes of voice generation time
10 minutes of transcription
Share project preview
Create Embed link
No credit card required
