Introduction to Voice Cloning
Murf offers advanced voice synthesis, editing, and visual timing features to help you create high-quality audio cloning in minutes.
Murf is a reliable online voice cloner that lets you easily clone your favorite actor’s voice. Murf ensures your cloned voices are safe and your team has exclusive access. But that’s not just all! Murf offers a complete voice solution. Murf offers advanced voice synthesis, editing, and visual timing features to help you create high-quality audio cloning in minutes.
Once you sign up with Murf, you will be assigned a dedicated account manager to guide you in every step of your deep voice cloning efforts. Your account manager will be your point of contact, from taking you through the user cycle to troubleshooting and support requirements.
With 24/7 instant access to your cloned voices, you are better poised to scale your content generation with ease. When you sign up for Murf for voice cloning services, you can access the entire Murf Studio. Use features like the soundtrack to add background music for your Audiobook or control pitch, speed, pause, and pronunciation to make your narration more relatable for your voice-over PPT. Simply upload your script and change it to audio using your custom voice clone. The possibilities are endless!
We do not currently provide self-served Voice Cloning solutions.
Contact our Enterprise Team today for more information.
🈂️🔃   Voice Cloning for Dubbing/Translation Use Cases
Our Murf Dub feature is a great option if you’re looking to clone a voice from an existing video or audio file in multiple languages. It lets you dub your content into different languages while preserving the original speaker’s voice.
