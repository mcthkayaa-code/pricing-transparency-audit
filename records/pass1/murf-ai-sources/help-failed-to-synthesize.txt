What does failed to synthesize mean?
There are a few reasons why this error would occur.
If your account has run out of Voice Generation Time.
If your payment has failed and your plan has not been renewed .
If an  invalid IPA  has been added to a particular word.
If an  unrecognized character  is present in your script. (Recommend: Paste text as plain text using Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + V)
