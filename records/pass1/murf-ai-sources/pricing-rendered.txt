=== FAQ ACCORDION ANSWERS (same page, expanded via click - permitted per protocol 6.3) ===

Q: What are Projects on Murf Studio?
A: Projects are where you seamlessly create, edit, customise, and share voiceovers in a
well-organised workflow. Each new project created consumes one project credit and the no. of
project credits available are subject to your plan's limit. For eg., a plan with 5 projects
indicates that a total of 5 projects can be saved within your workspace at any given time.

Q: How do you measure voice generation time?
A: Voice generation time is calculated as the sum of generated speech length of every text
block. It is consumed every time you render a newly created text block or modify text in an
existing text block. Modifying the generated speech using a different Voice actor, Style,
Pitch, Speed, Pause, Emphasis, Pronunciation, Punctuation and Volume for the same text will not
consume any voice generation time.

Q: How long can I use the free plan for?
A: There is no time limit to the free plan. You can use it until you exhaust your voice
generation time limit of 10 mins.

Q: Will I lose my free 10 mins if I upgrade to a paid plan?
A: No, your unused free minutes will not get lapsed. If you upgrade to a subscription plan,
your unused minutes would get added to your account under Additional minutes.

Q: What if I need more projects than I have in my plan limit?
A: To create more projects beyond your plan's limit, upgrade to Business/Enterprise Plan or buy
a plan with more projects. Alternatively, you can delete existing projects to create new
projects.

Q: Can I upgrade from a monthly to a yearly subscription?
A: Yes, you can do that anytime. If you have subscribed to a monthly plan, you will see an
Upgrade option on this page automatically.

Q: Will project credits be allocated every month?
A: No, Project credits are not allotted monthly. Regardless of the plan's duration, whether
monthly or yearly, the maximum number of projects in your workspace depends on the project
credits associated with your plan.

Q: Are the monthly limits applicable to yearly plans?
A: Yearly plans have no monthly limits, just an overall yearly limit. Let's say you take the
Basic Yearly Plan. Your voice generation time would be 24 hours/year, which means you could use
up more than 2 hours in months that you need more and less in others.

Q: Is the Voice Generation time shared among users on a plan with multiple users?
A: If you decide to go with a plan that has multiple users, don't worry! The voice generation
time will be shared among all of you, so everyone on the plan can use it.

Q: Can I cancel a subscription anytime?
A: Yes, absolutely. If you want to cancel your plan, simply go to 'Manage Billing' under your
profile and cancel. This will ensure that any future payments would not be deducted. If you
cancel, you will continue to have access to all the premium features until the end of your
current billing cycle.

Q: I am a registered Business. Can I use Murf's creator Plan?
A: As a registered business, it is ideal to opt for the Business Plan rather than the Creator
Plan. The Business Plan provides licensing for commercial rights tailored for businesses, making
it the ideal choice for your needs. [NOTE: advisory, not a stated prohibition -- Creator plan is
not documented as unavailable/restricted for businesses, only "not ideal"]

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: Payments already made against monthly or annual subscriptions would not be eligible for a
refund. If you have just bought an account and have an issue using it, please get in touch with
us.

Q: Do you charge sales or consumption taxes?
A: Certain US states are subject to a required sales or consumption tax. If your online order
includes sales tax, VAT, GST, or comparable consumption taxes, you will see the tax amount along
with the rate of tax applied in your shopping cart after the billing step and before checkout is
complete. [confirms taxes are excluded/separate per codebook first_charge_amount_usd rule 5]

Q: What is the difference between Personal and Business License?
A: Personal License: The Personal License permits individuals to use Murf for both personal and
commercial purposes on an individual basis. This license is suitable for personal projects and
small-scale commercial projects.
Business License: The Business License is designed for users representing businesses and
organizations. It is recommended for businesses to leverage Murf for creating a wide range of
content, both for internal and external purposes. This license is ideal for scaling up content
creation within a corporate or organizational context.
