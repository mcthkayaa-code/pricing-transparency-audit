How do credits work?
Credits are spent every time you produce an image in Recraft. An image is produced by
generating one from scratch or by modifying it using a tool like Modify Region or Remove
Background. Producing a single raster image uses 1 credit, and producing a single vector
image uses 2. Creating or converting an image into a mockup also uses 2 credits, while the
Creative Upscale tool uses 20.

Operation | Credits
Raster image - Generation of one raster image | 1
Raster image - Modify region | 1
Raster image - Crisp upscale | 1
Raster image - Erase region | 1
Raster image - Vectorization | 1
Raster image - Remove background | 1
Raster image - Replace background | 1
Vector image - Generation of one vector image | 2
Vector image - Modify region | 2
Vector image - Erase region | 2
Vector image - Remove background | 2
Vector image - Replace background | 2
Mockup - Convert image to mockup | 2
Mockup - Generate mockup image | 2
Creative upscale - Creative upscale | 20

What happens to unused credits?
Credits are reset daily for free users and monthly for subscribers. Top-up credits do not
expire. Top-up credit prices and conditions are experimental and subject to change.
