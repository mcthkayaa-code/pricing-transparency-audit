1. General Refund Policy
Overview of our refund terms.
This Refund Policy applies to all subscription services provided by M/s. Hyperleap Software Technologies Private Limited (&quot;Company&quot;, &quot;We&quot;, &quot;Us&quot;, &quot;Our&quot;) to You (&quot;User&quot;, &quot;Customer&quot;, &quot;You&quot;, &quot;Your&quot;).
This Refund Policy is incorporated by reference into our
Terms of Use
and
Payment Terms
.
Generally, we do not provide refunds for subscription services except as explicitly outlined in this policy or as required by applicable law.
Digital services — no physical returns:
Hyperleap AI is a digital software-as-a-service product. No physical goods are sold or shipped, so product returns and exchanges do not apply. The equivalent of a return is cancelling Your subscription — You retain access to paid features until the end of Your current billing period, after which access is restricted.

3. Exceptions to No-Refund Policy
When refunds may be provided.
Refund Request Window:
All refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of the original charge date. Requests submitted after this window are not eligible for review, except where a longer period is required by applicable law.
Legal Requirements
We provide pro-rated refunds where required by applicable consumer protection laws:

•
Australian Consumer Law
•
Canadian Consumer Protection Acts
•
US state consumer protection laws
•
Indian Consumer Protection Act
Service Failure
We may provide refunds at our discretion if:
✓

✓
Unable to deliver core functionality as described
✓
Material breach of service commitments
Other Exceptions
✓
Billing Errors:
We will correct and refund any overcharge
✓
Fraudulent Charges:
Refunds upon verification of unauthorized charges
4. Pro-Rated Refunds
How refunds are calculated.
Pro-rated refunds are only provided where legally required. Calculation method:
(Total paid ÷ Total days) × Remaining days = Refund amount
•
Minimum refund:
$5 USD or equivalent (refunds below this are not processed)
•
Processing time:
10-15 business days after approval
•
Refund method:
Original payment method used

Decision
Based on legal requirements, policy terms, and circumstances
