What happens when payments fail.
If a payment fails, We will:

Attempt to retry the payment automatically for up to 3 times over 7 days
•
Send You email notifications about the failed payment
•
Provide You an opportunity to update Your payment method
If payment is not collected within 7 days:
Your account may be suspended, access restricted, and data retained for 30 days. A late payment fee of $5 USD may be charged.

Invoicing
