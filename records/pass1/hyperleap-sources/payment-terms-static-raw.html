3. Billing Cycles and Payment Schedule

When and how you're billed.

Subscription fees are billed in advance based on Your selected billing cycle:

Monthly Billing

Charged monthly on the same calendar date each month

Annual Billing

Charged annually on the same calendar date each year

  • •Usage-based charges (if applicable) are calculated monthly and billed in arrears

Accepted payment methods.

Accepted Payment Methods

Tax responsibilities and additional charges.

All prices are exclusive of applicable taxes unless otherwise stated. You are responsible for:

  • •Value Added Tax (VAT)
  • •Goods and Services Tax (GST)
  • •Sales tax, Service tax
  • •Any other local, state, or federal taxes

Tax calculations are based on the billing address You provide. Payment processing fees, currency conversion fees, or international transaction fees charged by Your financial institution are Your responsibility.

6. Failed Payments and Account Suspension

What happens when payments fail.

If a payment fails, We will:

  • •Attempt to retry the payment automatically for up to 3 times over 7 days
  • •Send You email notifications about the failed payment
  • •Provide You an opportunity to update Your payment method
If payment is not collected within 7 days: Your account may be suspended, access restricted, and data retained for 30 days. A late payment fee of $5 USD may be charged.

7. Plan Changes and Upgrades

How to change your subscription.

You may upgrade or downgrade Your subscription plan at any time through Your account dashboard.

Upgrades

  • • Take effect immediately
  • • Prorated charge for remaining period
  • • Billing date remains the same

Downgrades

  • • Take effect at end of billing cycle
  • • No refund for current period
  • • Features restricted after cycle ends

  • •Annual prepayment discounts
  • •Custom billing cycles
  • •Purchase order processing
  • •Invoice-based billing

Contact our sales team at support@hyperleap.ai for special billing arrangements.

10. Invoicing, Data Retention & Security

Invoicing

Invoices and receipts are provided electronically via email and can be accessed from Your account dashboard. Invoice disputes must be raised within 30 days.

Data Retention

If suspended for non-payment, Your data is retained for 30 days. You can resolve payment issues, export data, or downgrade to a free plan during this period. After 30 days, data may be permanently deleted.

Payment Security

We do not store complete credit card information. All payments use industry-standard security: PCI-compliant processors, SSL/TLS encryption, and tokenization.
