Report malware

Squarespace Domains LLC and Squarespace Domains II LLC are committed to providing a safe and trusted service. If you have a concern about a domain name registered with Squarespace, you can submit a report to let us know.

Malware, or malicious software, are programs designed to disrupt, damage, or gain access to a computer without the owner's knowledge. Malware includes viruses, spyware, ransomware, and other unwanted software. Malware being used through multiple sources are referred to as botnets.

To learn more about reporting other suspicious domain activity to Squarespace, visit Reporting domain name abuse.

Before you begin:
• Ensure the domain you're reporting to us is registered and managed through Squarespace.
• Visit our Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy to review our policies about abuse and fraudulent or illegal activity.

Report phishing or pharming

Squarespace Domains LLC and Squarespace Domains II LLC are committed to providing a safe and trusted service. If you have a concern about a domain name registered with Squarespace, you can submit a report to let us know.

Phishing is a type of fraud that attempts to deceive users into sharing sensitive information like credit card information, passwords, or account details.

Where phishing attempts to deceive users into sharing sensitive information, pharming attempts to redirect users to fraudulent sites through modified/hijacked DNS, instead of where the user originally attempted to go.

To learn more about reporting other suspicious domain activity to Squarespace, visit Reporting domain name abuse.

Before you begin:
• Ensure the domain you're reporting to us is registered and managed through Squarespace.
• Visit our Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy to review our policies about abuse and fraudulent or illegal activity.

Non-Public Information Request Process Disclosure

For privacy reasons, Squarespace Domains LLC and Squarespace Domains II LLC (collectively, “Squarespace Registrar Entities” and each, a “Squarespace Registrar Entity”) generally redact customers’ personal contact information within registration data (“non-public registration data”) associated with general top-level domains for which a Squarespace Registrar Entity serves as the registrar of record (“domain name”) when a public lookup of such registration data through Squarespace’s Registration Data Directory Services (“RDDS”) outputs are performed.

However, in order to comply with ICANN’s Registration Data Policy, Squarespace Registrar Entities are obligated to allow third parties to submit a request for non-public registration data associated with a domain name and have such non-public registration data to be disclosed to them directly on the basis of demonstrable legitimate interest (each, a “request”). This page outlines (a) the format and content required for each request that must be submitted for Squarespace’s review, (b) how Squarespace will respond to your request and (c) the anticipated timeline for our response to your request. Only requests for single domains are permitted. Please note that this policy does not jeopardize the fundamental interests, rights and freedoms of a data subject, consistent with the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") or other similar privacy laws. It is also not a substitute for other less-intrusive mechanisms available and we expect these mechanisms to be exhausted prior to any request submitted hereunder, which request should only be submitted as an extreme, last resort measure given its potential impact to the data subject.

Points to consider


• If you are looking to only obtain non-public registration data in order to reach out to the contact associated with a domain name, Squarespace offers the ability to communicate through the form available here.

• If you are looking to report DNS abuse for a domain name registered through Squarespace, steps on how to do so are available here.

• If you believe you are potentially the victim of a crime associated to a domain name, you may file a complaint with the FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3), European Cybercrime Centre (EC3), or if you reside outside the foregoing regions, then your region’s equivalent cybercrime reporting authority.

• If you believe that a domain name registered at Squarespace violates your rights or if you believe that the domain Registrant is otherwise in breach of the representations made in Section 2 of the Uniform Domain Name Dispute Resolution Policy (UDRP), then you may file a complaint against the Registrant with an ICANN-approved dispute resolution provider (the “Resolution Provider”). You can find more information about the UDRP process, including how to file a complaint against the Registrant with an ICANN-approved dispute resolution provider, here.

• If you are a law enforcement agency seeking information, please note that we are unable to provide information about Squarespace accounts, users, or sites to law enforcement without a subpoena, court order, or warrant.

Once the receipt of the information and statements below have been acknowledged, Squarespace will review your submission and respond within 30 days from such acknowledgment absent exceptional circumstances. Squarespace Registrar Entity, in its sole discretion, may grant or deny requests and decisions are considered final. Repeated data access requests against our policy may result in rejection of any future requests you may submit.

Website refund policy

Last updated May 21, 2026 21:17

Sections

    Information about refunds for your website subscriptions.

    You can cancel your website subscription at any time. To request a refund, contact us by completing a refund request form.

    Refunds for Standard plans

    You can request a full refund for annual website subscriptions canceled within 14 days of purchase.

    We don’t offer refunds for:

    • Annual plans canceled after 14 days following the first payment
    • Monthly plans
    • Renewal payments - Cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged. You can find the renewal date on your invoices.
    • Lost free domain offers - If you cancel an annual plan within 14 days, the cost of the domain is deducted from your refund.

    Refunds for Mobile Start plans

    Mobile Start plans are purchased through the Squarespace app, which is managed by Apple. To request a refund for this plan, contact Apple.

    Refund policies for other Squarespace subscriptions

    Review the refund policies of other all other Squarespace subscriptions:

    • Acuity Scheduling refund policy
    • Digital products refund policy
    • Domains refund policy
    • Email Campaigns refund policy
    • Getty images refund policy
    • Google Workspace refund policy

    Also try: SEPA refund policies.
