Non-Public Information Request Process Disclosure For privacy reasons, Squarespace Domains LLC and Squarespace Domains II LLC (collectively, “Squarespace Registrar Entities” and each, a “Squarespace Registrar Entity”) generally redact customers’ personal contact information within registration data (“non-public registration data”) associated with general top-level domains for which a Squarespace Registrar Entity serves as the registrar of record (“domain name”) when a public lookup of such registration data through Squarespace’s Registration Data Directory Services (“RDDS”) outputs are performed. However, in order to comply with ICANN’s Registration Data Policy, Squarespace Registrar Entities are obligated to allow third parties to submit a request for non-public registration data associated with a domain name and have such non-public registration data to be disclosed to them directly on the basis of demonstrable legitimate interest (each, a “request”). This page outlines (a) the format and content required for each request that must be submitted for Squarespace’s review, (b) how Squarespace will respond to your request and (c) the anticipated timeline for our response to your request. Only requests for single domains are permitted. Please note that this policy does not jeopardize the fundamental interests, rights and freedoms of a data subject, consistent with the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") or other similar privacy laws. It is also not a substitute for other less-intrusive mechanisms available and we expect these mechanisms to be exhausted prior to any request submitted hereunder, which request should only be submitted as an extreme, last resort measure given its potential impact to the data subject. Points to consider • If you are looking to only obtain non-public registration data in order to reach out to the contact associated with a domain name, Squarespace offers the ability to communicate through the form available here. • If you are looking to report DNS abuse for a domain name registered through Squarespace, steps on how to do so are available here. • If you believe you are potentially the victim of a crime associated to a domain name, you may file a complaint with the FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) , European Cybercrime Centre (EC3) , or if you reside outside the foregoing regions, then your region’s equivalent cybercrime reporting authority. • If you believe that a domain name registered at Squarespace violates your rights or if you believe that the domain Registrant is otherwise in breach of the representations made in Section 2 of the Uniform Domain Name Dispute Resolution Policy (UDRP), then you may file a complaint against the Registrant with an ICANN-approved dispute resolution provider (the “Resolution Provider”). You can find more information about the UDRP process, including how to file a complaint against the Registrant with an ICANN-approved dispute resolution provider, here. • If you are a law enforcement agency seeking information, please note that we are unable to provide information about Squarespace accounts, users, or sites to law enforcement without a subpoena, court order, or warrant. Automatic subscription renewals Last updated August 10, 2026 14:14 Sections Review automatic payments for your Squarespace subscription. Squarespace subscriptions automatically renew for your convenience and to avoid any interruption of service. This guide covers how automatic renewal works and where to locate your renewal payment date. Tip : All Squarespace subscriptions are independent, and have their own billing cycle and invoice. To learn more, visit Checking your billing subscriptions . Which subscriptions automatically renew These subscriptions automatically renew on their own billing cycles unless you cancel the subscription. Click the links to learn more: Website subscriptions - Annual or monthly Squarespace Domains - After your initial registration term is over, we'll charge the payment method on file for another term. We’ll then continue to automatically renew your domain based on the cadence you chose. Email Campaigns - Annual or monthly Essential Email by Titan - monthly Digital Products - Annual or monthly Acuity Scheduling - Annual or monthly Google Workspace email addresses - Annual or monthly Mobile Start plans and Getty Image licenses are one-time purchases and don't auto-renew. How you'll be charged We'll charge the payment method on file for your site. Always keep your billing information updated to avoid billing failures and to ensure we charge the correct card. If your card on file has expired, we can still collect payment . If the wrong card was charged, we can't reprocess the charge on a different card. You'll receive an email reminder 15 days before annual website subscriptions renew so you'll have time to make any changes, unless you use SEPA. Monthly subscriptions renew automatically without an email reminder. To learn more about SEPA, review the section below. SEPA charges If you use SEPA as your payment method, you'll receive an email renewal reminder when we charge the renewal payment. If auto-renew is disabled for domains or you've canceled a subscription, we send an expiration reminder email instead. What happens if my subscription doesn't renew automatically If you have a subscription that doesn't renew automatically, your subscription will expire on the next payment date. If you'd rather end your subscription, you can cancel it at any time. When canceling outside the refund window, you'll continue to have service until the end of your billing cycle. If a subscription has been canceled and you have reached the end of your billing cycle, you can click Resubscribe to reactivate your subscription . Tip : To manage a domain subscription, open your domains dashboard , then click the domain you'd like to adjust. For more details specific to domains, visit Cancelling a Squarespace domain . Parked domains If your Squarespace domain isn't linked to an active website, it might be parked on a parking page . Parking pages are free, but your Squarespace domain renews automatically unless you disable auto-renew. To manage a parked domain: Open your domains dashboard . Click the domain name you want to cancel. Switch the Auto-renew toggle off. If your domain has a Reseller: Google message instead of an auto-renew toggle, you'll enable or disable auto-renew through your Google Workspace Admin console instead. Find your renewal payment dates Follow these steps to find the next renewal date for a subscription. Website Open the Billing panel . Under Subscriptions , click Website or Store , depending on the billing plan. The Payment due date is your next renewal date. Domain If your domain shares its billing information with a Squarespace website: Open the Billing panel . Under Subscriptions , click Domains , then click the domain name. If your domain doesn't share its billing information with a Squarespace website: Open your domains dashboard . Click the domain to open its domain details page. Your domain's renewal date appears in the Renews on section. Was this article helpful?
