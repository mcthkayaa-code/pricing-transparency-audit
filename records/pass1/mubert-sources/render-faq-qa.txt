Q: What is Mubert?
A: Mubert is one of the pioneers of AI-powered music creation, generating tracks from a database of sounds created by human producers. Our approach enhances the music quality while supporting artists and democratizing the commercial music creation and licensing process.

Q: Why are we different from competitors?
A: At Mubert, our main mission is to empower content creators with easy-to-license tool, powered by musicians. Human creativity is an inevitable part of music creation process.

Q: What is DSP?
A: A Digital Service Provider (DSP) is an online store (iTunes, Amazon Music) or streaming service (Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, YouTube Music) and etc.

Q: How to remove the watermark?

Q: What is the difference between the generation modes? (track/jingle/loop/mix)
A: – Track mode is the most native and is perfect for music of classic length– Jingle mode is best for music content up to 40 seconds long – these are short completed tracks with fixed and logical structure– Loop mode creates music pieces that are best looped – are best to use for NFTs or Instagram posts– Mix mode is perfect for any long-lasting content, mimicking the DJ's work tracks follow one after another, blending harmoniously – best for YT Channels or Podcasts

Q: May I generate tracks of 25 minutes + duration?
A: Mubert Render does not currently support generating individual tracks longer than 25 minutes. For longer continuous music, use Mix mode or contact us to learn more about Mubert API.

Q: How does the 'Search by reference' feature work?
A: Paste a link to a reference track and Mubert will analyze its musical characteristics to generate music inspired by a similar style, mood and energy — without copying the original composition.

Q: How is Quick Remix different from generating a new track?
A: Generating a new track creates a completely new composition based on your prompt. Quick Remix starts from an existing track and lets you regenerate or remove selected stems while preserving the overall character of the original track.

Q: How many tracks can I generate?
A: There are different monthly generation limits on different plans. The free Ambassador one allows to generate 25 tracks, while Creator & Pro up to 500 tracks, Business up to 1000.

If you're creating short-form videos, try Mubert Fuse to generate AI sound effects for Shorts and social media. Q: What is Quick Remix?
A: Quick Remix lets you customize an existing track by regenerating or removing individual stems while keeping the rest of the track unchanged. It's the fastest way to create new variations without starting from scratch.

Q: Which stems can I edit?
A: Quick Remix currently supports four stem groups: Drums, Bass, Lead and FX. You can regenerate or remove any combination of these stems.

Q: Can I regenerate and remove multiple stems at the same time?
A: Yes. You can select any combination of supported stems to regenerate or remove in a single remix.

Q: Can I preview my changes before generating a remix?
A: No. Selecting a stem for removal or regeneration does not change the current track immediately. Click Remix to generate a new version, and you will be able to preview it once generation is complete.

Q: Why don't I hear the changes immediately after removing or regenerating a stem?
A: Quick Remix does not edit the currently playing track in real time. Your selections are applied when you click Remix, which generates a new track version. Once generation is complete, you can preview, download, or remix the result again.

Q: Does Quick Remix change my original track?
A: No. Your original track always remains unchanged. Every remix is generated as a separate version.

Q: Can I remix Staff Picks tracks?
A: Yes. Quick Remix works with both your generated tracks and tracks from the Staff Picks library.

Q: Can I change the track duration in Quick Remix?
A: Yes. Changing track duration in Quick Remix is available on paid plans. Free users can remix a track using its original duration.

Q: Can I download separate stems or MIDI files?
A: Not yet. While Quick Remix lets you edit supported stems, exporting individual stems or MIDI files is not currently available.

Q: Can I remix a remix?
A: Yes. You can use Quick Remix on any remixed track and create as many consecutive versions as you need.

Q: Does Quick Remix use one generation from my monthly limit?
A: Yes. Each Quick Remix creates a new track version and uses one generation from your monthly limit.

Q: Can I undo a remix?
A: AnswerA remix cannot be undone, but your original track always remains unchanged. Every remix is saved as a separate version, so you can return to the original track at any time.

Q: Why does my remixed track sound different than expected?
A: Quick Remix generates a new version of the track rather than editing the original audio directly. Because music generation includes some variation, the regenerated stems may sound different from what you expected and can slightly affect the overall feel of the track. You can remix the result again to explore another variation.
