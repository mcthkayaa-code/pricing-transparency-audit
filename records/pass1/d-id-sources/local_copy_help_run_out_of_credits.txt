What happens when I run out of credits?

If you run out of credits and try to generate a video in the Studio, you'll see an upgrade popup with options to purchase more credits.

You have a few choices:
Upgrade your plan to a higher tier directly from the popup
Visit the Pricing page to explore and upgrade to a higher plan
Wait for your next billing renewal, when your credit balance will be automatically replenished based on your current subscription

Running low often? Upgrading to a higher plan gives you more monthly credits at a better rate.
