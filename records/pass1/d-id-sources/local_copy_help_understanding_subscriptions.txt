Understanding subscriptions: key information you should know

D-ID offers a range of subscription plans designed to fit different needs — from individuals to large-scale enterprises.

Available plans:
Free Trial – 14 days of unlimited use
Lite, Pro, Advanced – Paid Studio plans with increasing features and credits
Enterprise – Custom plan tailored for high-volume or advanced use cases

You can view a full feature comparison on our Pricing page.

Subscription types: Monthly vs. Annual

Annual Plans
Billed annually at a discounted monthly rate
Credits are issued monthly on your billing date
To get more monthly credits, upgrade to a higher-tier annual plan

Monthly Plans
Billed every month at the standard monthly rate
Credits are issued on your monthly billing date
You can increase your credits by upgrading to a higher monthly plan or switching to an annual plan

Studio vs. API Plans
Studio Plans - Ideal for users who prefer a visual interface to: Upload images / Type or upload scripts / Generate and manage videos easily
API Plans - Designed for developers who want to generate videos via code, with access to more programmatic flexibility. Each API plan comes with its own pricing, credit allocation, and features.

For detailed subscription plan information, visit our pricing pages here: Studio Pricing / API Pricing
