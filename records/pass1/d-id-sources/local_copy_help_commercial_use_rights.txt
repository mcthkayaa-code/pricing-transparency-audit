What are the copyright and commercial use rights for my videos?

Your rights depend on the plan you're subscribed to, and each plan includes its own license and moderation settings — you can view the full details on our Pricing page.

Assuming you own the rights to the image, audio, and text you upload:

Trial, Lite, Build – For personal use only
You can share your videos on social media, but they can't be used commercially.

Pro, Advanced, Enterprise, Launch, Scale – For commercial use
You're free to use your videos in ads, marketing, business content, and more.

For full terms and conditions read our Terms & Conditions
