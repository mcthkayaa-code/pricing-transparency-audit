| Tier | Monthly x 12 | Stated annual | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus | 204 | 200 | 4 |
| Max | 1020 | 1000 | 20 |
| Generative | 2040 | 2000 | 40 |
| Elite | 10800 | 10800 | 0 (exact) |

The pattern is consistent across the whole grid, not an isolated anomaly on the flagged entry
tier: the vendor sets the annual figure as the round marketing number (200/1000/2000/10800) and
the "/mo" figure is derived from it, not the other way around. This is vendor pricing design, not
a misread.
