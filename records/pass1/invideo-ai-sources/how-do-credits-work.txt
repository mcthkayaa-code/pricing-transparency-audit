How Do Credits Work?
What is a credit?
Credits work like currency on invideo. They're used for video creation, generative models, and
premium features. Every paid plan includes a monthly credit allowance that resets each billing
cycle.

What can I spend credits on?
Video generation — all supported video models at their original API pricing.
Generative models — premium image, audio, and agent workflows not covered by Unlimited.
Additional premium features — certain workflows may charge credits per use.

You're not charged credits for downloading content you've already generated, for editing existing
videos, or for image generations on plans that include Unlimited.

PLAN            CREDITS/MO
Free            Limited
Plus            75
Max             390
Generative      800
Elite           4,250
Team Standard   160/seat
Team Premium    1,600/seat
Enterprise      Custom

When do credits reset?
Your credit allowance resets at the start of each billing cycle, which begins on the day you
subscribed (not the first of the calendar month). For example, if you subscribed on the 14th, your
credits reset on the 14th of every month.
Unused credits don't roll over. Whatever you haven't used by the end of your billing cycle resets
to your plan's monthly allowance.

Am I charged per generation?
Yes. Credits are charged differently for video creation, generative models, and premium features.
The cost depends on the model, the output length, and the resolution. You can see the credit cost
before you confirm each generation.

What if I run out of credits mid-cycle?
You have two options: upgrade your plan for a higher monthly allowance, or buy a one-time credit
pack. Purchased credit packs are added instantly and can be used across all features.

TYPE            DETAILS                                        ROLLS OVER?
Plan credits    Allocated monthly with your subscription        No — resets each billing cycle
Top-up credits  Purchased on-demand when you need more           No — resets each billing cycle
