Model & agent prices are subject to change. We update pricing as underlying models change; the
exact credit cost for your selected model is shown in the app before you confirm the generation.

How the range works
Models with multiple tiers (resolution, quality, or aspect ratio) show a credits/image range. The
minimum is the cheapest tier (e.g. 1K resolution, low quality); the maximum is the most expensive
tier (e.g. 4K resolution, high quality). You'll see the exact cost for your chosen configuration
before you confirm.

What counts as one image?
One image = one generation from one prompt. Multi-image outputs (e.g. 4-up grids) are billed per
image. Reference image inputs don't add to the cost.
