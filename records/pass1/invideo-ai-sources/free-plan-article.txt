Can I use invideo AI for free?
Updated over a week ago

Yes, you can use invideo AI for free without entering your card details.

The free plan gives you limited credits to explore the platform. You can use these credits across
invideo's features including the Agents & Models flow for image generation.

Note: This quota on the Free plan is reset weekly on Monday at 12 a.m. UTC.

What you can do on the free plan
Access to AI models with limited credits
Generate images using the Agents & Models flow
Use and share videos on social media
