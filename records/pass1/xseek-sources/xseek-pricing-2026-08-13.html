FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Find answers to common questions about generative engine optimization and our platform.

Another question? Write to us
What is generative engine optimization?
It's SEO for AI answers: structuring your content, data, and authority so ChatGPT, AI Overview, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Grok cite you when people ask questions in your industry.
How is traffic shifting from Google to ChatGPT?
Users ask their questions directly to AI instead of clicking 10 blue links. Result: Google organic traffic flattens or drops, but AI answers influence the purchase decision. Your bet becomes being cited in the answer, not just ranking on Google.
Can I try your app for free?
We prefer an advice-first approach: book a 30-minute call with a strategist. We look at your AI profile live and tell you honestly whether xSeek is for you. No generic demo.
How does xSeek compare brands and generate LLM rankings?
We query AIs with your strategic prompts on a recurring cadence, identify who gets cited, on what tone, for which topics. We cross-reference with the sources AIs use to generate answers. That becomes your share of voice in AI.
How does xSeek track AI citations of my content?
Each AI exposes its sources differently: links in Perplexity, citations in Claude, search grounding in Gemini. We capture them all and alert you when your domain is cited, mentioned, or ignored.
How is AI SEO different from traditional SEO?
SEO optimizes for 10 links. AEO (or GEO) optimizes for a single synthesized answer. You no longer win by keyword volume — you win by clarity, authority and information structure.
What is the xSeek CLI and how can it automate my AI SEO?
A command-line tool to audit your site, generate an llms.txt, validate your AI robots.txt, and run analyses in CI/CD. Perfect for technical teams shipping AEO-ready content without the UI.
Where can I see your product tour video?
We intentionally chose not to put a public demo video. We prefer to show you the platform with your own data live during a 15-minute call.
