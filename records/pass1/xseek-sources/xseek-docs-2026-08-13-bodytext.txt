Getting started
5 steps · 10 min
Welcome to xSeek. This walkthrough takes you from signup to your first dashboard of real visibility data.
01
Create an account
Sign up by visiting the signup page. Choose the plan that fits your needs and complete registration.
i
Tip.
Compare plans to find the best fit — Starter for solo operators, Growth for teams, Scale for agencies.
02
Add your website
From the dashboard, add your website URL. We verify ownership through Google Search Console integration or a meta-tag check. Takes under a minute.
03
Set up your first keywords
Add keywords relevant to your business. We'll start monitoring your rankings across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and other LLMs.
!
Note.
Tracked keywords depend on your plan — from 3 on Starter to unlimited on Scale.
04
Review your dashboard
Your dashboard populates with initial data right away. Full baseline metrics across all engines take 24–48 hours to settle.
05
Create AI agents with
MCP.
Set up automated AI agents using our Model Context Protocol integration. Configure Claude Code or other assistants to continuously monitor and optimize your AI search performance — on autopilot.
i
Install the xSeek CLI with
curl -fsSL https://cli.xseek.io/install.sh | sh
to register it as an MCP server with Claude Code: 19 tools, 8 resources, ready in one curl.
Key features
4 pillars
xSeek offers a comprehensive suite of tools to monitor and improve your website's performance in AI search results.
LLM real-time tracking
Monitor your website's ranking across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and more — with real-time data, not weekly snapshots.
AI-powered
insights
Receive intelligent recommendations to optimize your content for better performance in AI search — prioritized by visibility lift.
Competitor analysis
Compare your AEO performance against competitors. See exactly where they rank, what they're cited for, and where you can take share.
Content optimization
Specific, page-level recommendations for improving visibility in AI search results — what to write, what to restructure, what to retire.
How it works
Pipeline
xSeek uses advanced technology to track and analyze your website's performance in AI search engines. Four stages, fully automated.
1
Data collection
We query LLMs with your target keywords and analyze where your site appears — mentions, rankings, and surrounding context.
2
Analysis
Our AI identifies patterns, opportunities, and issues affecting your performance in AI search results.
3
Recommendations
You get actionable recommendations to improve content, structure, and AI ranking strategy. Prioritized, not dumped.
4
Monitoring
Continuous monitoring with regular reports and alerts — so you know the moment something moves.
