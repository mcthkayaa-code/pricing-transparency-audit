What you get
Six reasons this isn't
just another CLI.
Claude Code built-in
Claude Code runs inside the app with your xSeek data already connected — no tokens to juggle, no environment to configure.
Content Studio sync
Generate articles in the desktop and push them directly to your Content Studio. Draft, review, publish — same place.
Brand voice
aware.
Uses your brand voice, audiences, and knowledge base automatically. No prompt engineering for tone — it's already there.

Review & comment
