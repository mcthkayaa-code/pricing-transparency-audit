The basics
What is AI visibility tracking?
AI visibility tracking measures how often, and how favorably, AI answer engines mention your brand when buyers ask about your category. It's the AI-era version of rank tracking, except instead of a position on Google, you're watching whether AI names you at all.
Because AI answers are personalized and change constantly, one manual check tells you nothing. Real tracking runs the same buyer questions on a schedule, across every engine, and shows the trend, who is gaining, who is losing, and where you stand.

FAQ
AI visibility tracking, answered.
What is an AI visibility tool?
An AI visibility tool tracks how AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini mention your brand, how often, how favorably, and versus competitors, so you can improve your presence in AI search.
What are the best AI visibility tools?
The best AI visibility tools go beyond a dashboard: they track every major engine, compare you to competitors, and tell you what to do next. xSeek is built around that action-first approach for marketing teams.
How do you track brand visibility in AI?
By running the questions your buyers ask across each AI engine on a schedule, recording whether you're mentioned, how, and versus whom, then trending it over time. Manual checks don't work because AI answers are personalized and change constantly.
Which AI engines does xSeek track?
ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and other answer engines, plus the AI crawlers visiting your site.
Is AI visibility tracking accurate?
Accuracy comes from running enough prompts on a schedule rather than one-off checks. xSeek samples consistently across engines so you see a real trend, not noise.
Do I need to be technical?
No. xSeek gives marketing teams a clear, prioritized plan, not raw data to interpret.
