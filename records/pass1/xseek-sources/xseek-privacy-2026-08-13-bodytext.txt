Last updated: February 19, 2026
What is xSeek?
xSeek helps businesses understand how AI models discover, crawl, and cite their websites. We track AI bot visits, monitor citations in AI-generated responses, and provide insights into your AI search visibility.
What We Collect
Account Information
Email address and name when you sign up
Organization name and website URLs you add for tracking
Website Analytics
AI bot visit data (bot name, URLs visited, timestamps)
Citation data from AI model responses
Prompt configurations you create
Usage Data
How you interact with our dashboard
Feature usage patterns
How We Use Your Data
Provide AI visibility tracking and analytics
Display leaderboards and citation sources
Improve our service and develop new features
Send product updates (you can opt out)
Google User Data
xSeek integrates with Google APIs to provide search performance insights. This section describes how we handle data received from Google APIs, in accordance with the
Google API Services User Data Policy
, including the Limited Use requirements.
What Google Data We Access
When you connect your Google account, we request read-only access to:
Google Search Console data
— search queries, impressions, clicks, and average position for your verified properties
Google Analytics data
— traffic metrics and page-level analytics for your connected properties
Basic profile information
— your name and email address, used to identify your account
How We Use Google Data
Google user data is used solely to:
Display your search performance metrics within the xSeek dashboard
Correlate AI visibility data with organic search performance
Generate reports that combine AI citation data with search analytics
Sharing, Transfer & Disclosure of Google Data
We do
not
share, transfer, or disclose Google user data to any third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:
Infrastructure providers
— Your data is stored on our hosting and database providers (e.g., Vercel, Neon) solely to operate the service. These providers act as data processors and do not have independent access to your Google data.
Legal obligations
— We may disclose data if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process (e.g., a court order or subpoena).
With your explicit consent
— We will not share your Google data with any other party unless you explicitly authorize it.
We do
not
sell Google user data. We do
not
use Google user data for advertising. We do
not
allow humans to read your Google data except where necessary to provide support you requested, to comply with legal obligations, or for security purposes such as investigating abuse.
Data Storage & Retention
Google user data is stored securely in our database with the same encryption and security measures applied to all user data. OAuth tokens are stored server-side and never exposed to the client. You can disconnect Google at any time from your Organization Settings, which revokes our access and deletes stored tokens.
Limited Use Disclosure
The use of raw or derived user data received from Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the
Limited Use requirements
.
xSeek's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the
Google API Services User Data Policy
, including the Limited Use requirements.
What We Don't Do
We don't sell your data
We don't share individual website analytics with third parties
We don't use Google user data for advertising or any purpose unrelated to providing xSeek's features
We don't access your website content beyond what's publicly available
Data Retention
Account data: kept while your account is active
Analytics data: retained for 2 years
You can request deletion at any time
Your Rights
You can:
Access your data
Export your data
Delete your account and associated data
Opt out of marketing emails
Security
We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely.
Cookies
We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We also use analytics cookies to understand how you use xSeek, which helps us improve the product.
Third-Party Services
We use the following categories of third-party service providers to operate xSeek. These providers act as data processors and only access data as necessary to perform their services:
Hosting & infrastructure
— for running our application and storing data
Payment processing
— for handling subscriptions and billing
Email delivery
— for transactional and product update emails
Analytics
— for understanding aggregate usage patterns
None of these providers receive your Google user data for their own independent use. Google user data is only processed by infrastructure providers as necessary to store and serve it back to you within xSeek.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or through the xSeek dashboard.
