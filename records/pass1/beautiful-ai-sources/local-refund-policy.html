What is your refund policy?

Fatima
Updated 3 months ago

Annual subscribers may be able to receive a partial refund** if you submit a request within 24 hours of your initial purchase or renewal of your subscription. Any cancelation (trial or renewal) requested after payment has gone through will be charged the full monthly amount of that period.

Monthly subscriptions are not refundable.

**All refunds are subject to a processing fee

For more information, or to request a refund, contact support@beautiful.ai.
