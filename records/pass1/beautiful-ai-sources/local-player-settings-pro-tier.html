Player Settings (Pro Tier)

Ken. Updated 3 months ago.

Plan Availability: Pro

When you share or embed a link to your presentation, you are sharing what we call a "Player". The Player allows you to adjust what to expose to your viewers. You can:

  • Show the title of your presentation
  • Show the author of the presentation
  • Allow social sharing
  • Add an option to allow users to copy your presentation to their libraries
  • Auto-advance your slides
  • Loop your presentation (Please note, when set to ON, this will not track analytics)

Customize Player Settings: Click on Actions. Select the Player Settings tab.
