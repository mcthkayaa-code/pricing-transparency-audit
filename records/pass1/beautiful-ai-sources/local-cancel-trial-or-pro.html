How do I cancel my subscription (Trial or Pro)?

Fatima. Updated 5 months ago.

You may cancel your Beautiful.ai subscription by downgrading your account to our Basic plan. Note, you must use a desktop/laptop device in order to cancel.

  1. Log into your account at the main website (https://www.beautiful.ai/login).
  2. Once you're logged in, click the Profile icon at the top right. Then, select Manage Account
  3. Click the Billing tab
  4. Then click on 'Manage Plan'
  5. You will be able to choose to cancel either your trial if still currently trialing or your Pro subscription
  6. When you cancel your subscription, you can use Beautiful.ai until the end of your billing cycle.
