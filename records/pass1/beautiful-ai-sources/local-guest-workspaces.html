Guest Workspaces

Fatima. Updated 3 months ago.

Anyone without a paid seat is considered a Guest: with streamlined access, upgraded permissions, and the tools they need to collaborate effectively. And yes, no more watermarks.

What is a Workspace Guest?

A Guest is a user who has been invited to collaborate on a presentation but does not have a paid seat in the workspace.

What Guests Can Do:

  • Unlimited slides
  • View or edit presentations they've been invited to
  • Use premium features available in your plan, such as: AI Rewrite, Custom Fonts, Team Resources
  • Collaborate in real time
  • Export and share presentations without watermarks
  • Be added to folders

What Guests Can't Do:

  • Create, import, or duplicate presentations
  • See the full workspace library. Guests must be explicitly invited to a presentation to view it.
  • Invite other guests to the workspace
  • Modify workspace-wide settings, themes, or team resources
