Cookies

A cookie is a string of information that a website stores on a visitor’s computer, and that the visitor’s browser provides to the website each time the visitor returns.

Cookies and similar technologies (e.g. Local Storage) on our Platform allow us to authenticate you, to track your browsing behavior, links clicked, items purchased, your device type, about how you use and interact with our Platform and or to run our Affiliate Program. This allows us to provide you with more relevant product offerings and advertisement, a better experience with our products and services, and to collect, analyze and improve the performance of our Platform. We may also collect IP address to enhance our platform security. Note that cookies may be placed by Third Party Services that we use (e.g. Google Analytics). You can choose to turn off all cookies at any time from your browser settings. If you turn cookies off, some features will be disabled such as not being able to log in to dashboard.
