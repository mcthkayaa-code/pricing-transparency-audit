Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Phrasly.

Are humanizations really unlimited?

Absolutely. Unlike other tools that advertise 'unlimited' words but implement hidden caps, Phrasly delivers true unlimited processing. Our users regularly process millions of words monthly at a single flat rate - no tricks, no hidden limits.

Will my texts be watermarked in the future?

No. Here's why: Phrasly has built its own AI models, trained on over a million pages of real human writing. Other tools just use GPT-3 or GPT-4, which means when OpenAI adds watermarks, their texts will become detectable. Since we control our own technology, this isn't a risk with Phrasly. Our testing across 100,000+ documents shows an average human score of 99.7%.

What are content generation credits?

You get 15 monthly credits to generate papers, blog posts, or other content that you specify. Our system searches Google and Bing in real-time for citations. We limit this to 15 generations because scanning the web and verifying sources takes a lot of computing power, as Phrasly's crawlers scan the web in real-time.
