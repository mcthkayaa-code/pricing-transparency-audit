What is Revid.ai?
Revid.ai is the #1 AI video generator -- a platform that turns text, prompts, articles, music,
and images into ready-to-publish short-form videos for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. It
combines cutting-edge models (Google Veo3, OpenAI Sora 2, ElevenLabs voices in 70+ languages,
Flux/Seedream/GPT-Image visuals) with 100+ specialized tools, a full timeline editor, direct
social publishing, and daily automation. More than 240,000 short videos have been created by
14,000+ creators across 68 countries. [...]

What AI models generate the visuals?
"AI video per 5 seconds, billed pro rata per second: Base (15 credits), Pro (50), Ultra (100;
Seedance 1080p 250, Seedance 4K 500), Google Veo3 (100), OpenAI Sora 2 (130). AI images per
image: Base (1), Pro (4), Ultra (16). Plus stock footage, your own uploads, and gameplay
backgrounds. [...]"

What voices and languages are supported?
"70+ languages via ElevenLabs [...] You can clone a voice, design one from a text description
(Elite plan), or record yourself with the built-in teleprompter."
-- NOTE: this answer references an "Elite plan" for voice design, one more reference to the
plan name absent from the live pricing grid.

Can I create talking avatar videos?
"[...] generate a lifelike presenter (40/100/200 credits per 500 characters depending on
quality). [...] Face Swap (motion transfer) swaps a face in existing footage (100 credits per
5 seconds)."

>>> What subscription plans are available? <<<
"Growth: $39/month (2,000 credits) -- the most popular. Elite: $89/month (5,000 credits, voice
cloning, 5 Auto-Mode workers). Ultra: $199/month (12,000 credits, 10 Auto-Mode workers). Yearly
billing is roughly 17% cheaper. All paid plans include the 100+ tools, 70+ language voiceovers,
direct publishing, and full API/MCP/CLI access. High-volume plans go up to 100,000 credits/month."

>>> How do credits work and what do things cost? <<<
"Credits are consumed by generation: AI images 1-16 credits, AI video 15-130 credits per 5
seconds depending on the model (up to 500 for 4K Seedance, billed pro rata per second), voice
10 credits per 500 characters, avatars 40-200 credits per 500 characters, music generation 20
credits, 4K export 10 credits. A typical 30-second video with Pro moving images costs roughly
40-80 credits. Estimate any render for free before paying."

Can I buy extra credits?
"Yes: Credit Boost packs of 1,000 ($49), 4,000 ($99), 10,000 ($199), and 100,000 ($1,900) stack
on top of plan credits. Supported auto top-up packs can also be purchased programmatically via
the API or MCP."
-- These are OPTIONAL top-ups ("stack on top of plan credits"), not a required charge.

What is Auto-Mode?
"Auto-Mode workers are always-on pipelines [...] 3 workers on standard plans, 5 on Elite, 10 on
Ultra." -- again names "Elite", not present on the live pricing page (which instead shows
Growth at "3 Auto-Mode Workers" and Ultra at "10 Auto-Mode Workers", matching Ultra's figure but
with Growth taking the position this FAQ assigns to the (here-absent) base "standard" plans).

What export options are available?
"720p, 1080p, or 4K (beta, 10 credits) at 30 or 60 fps, MP4 or GIF, three compression levels,
and optional AI upscaling (5 credits/second) for AI-generated clips. [...]"
