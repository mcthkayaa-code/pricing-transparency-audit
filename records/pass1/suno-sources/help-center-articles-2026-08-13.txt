URL: https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2643713
TITLE: "What happens if I use all of my paid credits?"
EDITED: Friday, December 19 2025
================================================================================
If you're using a Pro or Premier plan, credits are replenished once monthly at the same time
as your original purchase. For example, if you subscribed on July 4th at 3pm local time, your
next replenishment will happen at 3pm local time on August 4th. If you run out of monthly
credits, you will receive the same 50 credit daily allotment as our Basic plan users, starting
on the next day and running to the end of the billing cycle. When a new cycle begins, your
monthly allotment will be used first, and the 50 daily credits will not reach your account
until all monthly credits are used. Beyond the 50 extra daily credits, you can purchase
additional credits by visiting suno.com/account then choosing Buy More Credits to purchase
add-on credits to be used while you are subscribed.
Note: Songs made with the 50 credits per day will still be granted commercial use rights, as
long as you are still subscribed. Once your membership reverts to the Basic tier, any new
songs will not be eligible for commercial use.

URL: https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2550209
TITLE: "Can I get a refund?"
EDITED: Friday, December 19 2025
================================================================================
There are a few key areas in our Terms of Service regarding refunds.
From "Your Registration Obligations": "If we discontinue the Service, we will refund you any
pre-paid fees on a pro rata basis, based upon the number of months left in the pre-payment
period." This indicates that we will offer a refund in cases where we discontinue service. As
noted, the refund will be prorated based on the remaining subscription duration.
From "Refunds and Cancellations": "Payments made by you hereunder are final and
non-refundable, unless otherwise determined by Suno." These are refunds made on a case-by-case
basis. Some examples of reasons we would honor a refund would include:
- Accidental payment: if you paid for a yearly subscription instead of a month, we can help to
  revert your payment to a monthly plan. This would include a partial refund.
- Accidental/unattended sign up: If you started a subscription but didn't use it at all. This
  can be tricky to determine; but if there are zero songs in your account and we see one or
  more payments, we're likely to assist with a refund.
- Product malfunction: If you experience significant downtime due to an outage or feature
  that isn't working, we'll go above and beyond to offer credits to make up for it. Unlike
  monthly credits, these will carry over to the next month!
Some cases where we would not honor a refund:
- Changed your mind: You've used Suno for six months, and decided now that you don't like it.
  In this case, our team will recommend you cancel your plan. You can continue to use the
  service through the end of the billing cycle, and the account will revert to the Basic plan
  upon completion. If it's only been a day or two, give our team a shout at support@suno.com
  and we'll do our best to make it right!
- Intermittent usage: If you've had a monthly plan and used Suno on and off for several
  months, we cannot issue a refund for the time you didn't use it. Instead, we encourage you
  to cancel anytime you won't be using Suno, and resubscribe when you're ready to use more than
  the Basic plan credit allotment!

EDITED: Wednesday, December 17 2025

Songs made on the free plan are only intended for personal, non-commercial use. These songs
cannot be monetized, but can be shared using your Suno link. On the free plan, you can:
- Create using 50 credits that renew daily
- Keep the tracks set to Link-Only, or Publish them to your Suno page
- Share your songs using the link to your song page, playlist, etc.
- Use the song in a personal, non-monetizing project
As always, any material on Suno is subject to our Terms of Service and published tracks are
subject to our Community Guidelines.

URL: https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9601665
TITLE: "What rights do I have with a paid subscription?"  [category: Rights & Ownership > Rights]
EDITED: Wednesday, December 17 2025
================================================================================
Songs made while subscribed are granted commercial use rights, in addition to your right to
use them for personal, non-commercial purposes. Commercial use rights allow you to monetize
the songs however you wish, without Suno claiming a stake in the earnings. With a paid
subscription, you can:
- Use any amount of your monthly allotment of credits (note that annual plans still drop
  credits on a monthly basis)
- Download your tracks for use outside of Suno
- Monetize your songs
- Distribute them to streaming platforms
- Use them in film, tv, or video games
- Sell them independently (such as selling an album via BandCamp, etc)
Keep in mind, granting commercial use rights does not guarantee copyright protection!
Copyright qualification and protection is determined by your region/country's copyright
office, not by Suno.
FINDING: No mention of watermarks anywhere in this article. Confirms credit allotment is
monthly even under annual billing (relevant to cost-per-output period basis).

URL: https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2410049
TITLE: "What type of plan do I have?"  [category: Accounts & Billing > Accounts]
EDITED: Friday, December 19 2025
================================================================================
Suno offers three plan types, two of which are paid subscriptions. The Free plan offers 50
credits that replenish daily. This is enough to make 10 songs per day! Pro is the entry option
for paid subscriptions. With a Pro subscription, you receive an allotment of 2500 credits each
month. Premier is our highest credit-per-month plan available. With a Premier plan, you
receive 10,000 credits each month. You can compare the plans anytime by visiting
suno.com/account. Need more credits? Pro and Premier plans also have the option to purchase
more credits. Once you're signed up, you'll see the Buy More Credits button in the area linked
above. Pro and Premier users who reach their monthly allotment will now receive up to 50
credits per day after all monthly credits have been used!
FINDING: Independently corroborates Pro = entry paid tier and the 2,500/10,000 monthly credit
figures from the pricing page. No credit-to-song conversion rate stated.

URL: https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2524993
TITLE: "Why did my credits reset?"  [category: Accounts & Billing > Troubleshooting]
EDITED: Friday, December 19 2025
================================================================================
Yes, paid subscriptions still have expiring credits! If you're using the Basic (free) plan,
you'll note that you get up to 50 new credits replenished daily. This means if you sign up on
Saturday and start with 50 credits, no matter how many you use, you will start with 50 credits
again on Sunday. If you use 50 credits Saturday, you will start with 50 on Sunday. If you use
10 credits on Saturday, you will still start with 50 credits on Sunday! The same concept
applies with our Pro and Premier subscription options, but with a monthly replenishment. Pro
subscribers receive 2500 credits per month. Whether you use 10 credits or all 2500, your next
billing cycle will start with 2500 credits. Premier subscribers receive 10,000 credits per
month. No matter how many credits were used, the next billing cycle will start with 10,000
credits. This information can also be found by visiting suno.com/account.
FINDING: Direct, explicit, official statement that unused credits (free AND paid tiers) do
NOT carry over between periods — each period resets to the flat allotment regardless of what
was left unused. Primary evidence for credit_rollover_policy.
