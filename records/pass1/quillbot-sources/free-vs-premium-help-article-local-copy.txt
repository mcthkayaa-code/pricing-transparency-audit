What's the difference between a free and a Premium account?
2 months ago Updated

A free Quillbot account includes basic access to our tools, while a Premium account unlocks expanded options and advanced features. Here's a quick comparison.

Free account

Free accounts include:
Standard and Fluency modes
3 Synonyms settings
Paraphrasing up to 125 words
Summarizing up to 1,200 words
Access to extensions
20 AI Chat queries/day
3 image generations/day
3 AI presentation generations/day

Premium account
Premium accounts include everything in Free, plus higher limits and exclusive features:
