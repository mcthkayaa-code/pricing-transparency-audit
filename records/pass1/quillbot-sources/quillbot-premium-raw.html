FAQs

What happens if I pause my subscription?
If you aren't going to use your Premium subscription for a while (if you’re on summer vacation, for example), you can go to your Account page and pause it. You won’t be billed for that time. No need to cancel.
How do I pay for a Premium subscription?
You can pay using a credit/debit card or with your PayPal account. Please click

here

 to explore the plans.
Can I use Quillbot Premium on multiple devices?
You can use Premium on all your devices. To prevent abuse, you can only use it on 10 devices at a time.
