"Buy Without Auto-Renewal
You can buy a Subscription without Auto-Renewal. Clicking the Buy button, you get to the
Checkout page. On the Checkout page, you click the Auto-renewal link. After clicking the
link, you will see the dialog on which you can click the Switch to Manual Renewal link.

Cancel Auto-Renewal At Any Time
You can cancel the auto-renewal of your Premium Plan at any time by following the steps.
Open the Licences and Subscription page from your user Profile. Click the Unsubscribe
link to cancel Auto-renewal."

READING: at checkout, Auto-Renewal is presented as the default state on the "Auto-renewal
link" - the buyer must take an affirmative extra step ("Switch to Manual Renewal") to opt
out. This is the basis for auto_renewal_default = "on", sourced from this document
(higher-ranked than the pricing page for this variable class). Also confirms
cancellation_self_serve = self_serve_documented (Profile > Licenses and Subscriptions >
Unsubscribe link; no contact/ticket/email required), consistent with the pricing-page
FAQ's answer to "Can I cancel my Premium Plan at any time?".
