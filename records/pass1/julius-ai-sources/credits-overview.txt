Payments & Billing / Understanding credits
Learn how credits work in Julius, how they're consumed, and how many you get with each plan.

What are credits?
Instead of counting messages, Julius now measures usage in credits. Different tasks cost
different amounts of credits depending on the compute involved. A simple message costs
less than running a full analysis or executing code.
This change lets us give you access to frontier AI models, larger context windows, and
better compute environments without raising prices. In our testing, Julius is up to 7x
more capable on complex, multi-step tasks under the new credit system. For most users
(roughly 95%), day-to-day usage feels the same — just with noticeably better results.

Credits by plan
Plan | Monthly credits
Free | 25 welcome bonus + 25 daily credits
Plus | 2,000
Pro | 5,000
Max ($200) | 25,000
Max ($500) | 70,000
Business ($450) | 60,000
Business ($750) | 100,000
Business ($1,400) | 200,000
Business ($2,500) | 400,000
Max comes at two levels and Business at four: the higher price unlocks more monthly
credits. Paid-plan credits renew each billing cycle. The Free plan's 25 welcome bonus
credits are a one-time grant and don't renew.
Annual plans are billed once per year at a discounted rate, with all credits granted
upfront. See julius.ai/pricing for current pricing.

How credits are consumed
Not every action costs the same number of credits. A few things affect the cost:
- Action type — a simple message costs less than running a full analysis or executing code
- AI model — more capable models use more credits
- Complexity — longer outputs, bigger datasets, and multi-step tasks add up faster
You can track your credit usage in real time on the Usage page.

Q: How many credits does a single message cost?
A: There's no single answer — cost depends on the message complexity and which tools are
   invoked (e.g., web search, slides, financial APIs). A quick question will cost far less
   than a multi-step analysis that pulls data, runs code, and generates a deliverable.

Q: Does model selection affect credit usage?
A: Yes — model choice is one of the biggest drivers of credit consumption. Faster models
   like Julius 1.2 Lite and Claude Sonnet 5 consume credits far more slowly than frontier
   models like Julius 1.2 Max, Claude Opus 5, Claude Fable 5, Grok 4.5, GPT-5.6, and Muse
   Spark 1.1. If you want to stretch your credits further on simpler tasks, pick a lighter
   model.

Q: How do I check my credit balance?
A: You can view your current balance, credit grants, and usage history at any time on the
   Usage page.

Q: What happens to my existing coupons or discounts?
A: Any coupons attached to your subscription will continue to be honored under the new
   credit system. Your pricing and discounts carry over unchanged.

Q: When are credits granted?
A: Monthly subscribers get their credits when their subscription starts, and again each
   time their billing cycle renews. Annual subscribers receive all their credits upfront
   when their subscription starts or renews. Free plan users receive a one-time grant of
   100 credits when they sign up; these credits don't renew.
   [NOTE: this directly conflicts with the "Credits by plan" table above, which states
   Free = "25 welcome bonus + 25 daily credits" (implying a recurring daily grant, not a
   single one-time 100-credit grant). Both statements are on this same official document.
   See conflict_note in the record.]

Q: Do unused credits roll over?
A: No. Unused credits expire at the end of your billing cycle and don't carry over.

Q: Can I buy more credits?
A: At the moment, purchasing additional credits or topping up your balance is not
   supported. If you need more usage right away, you may consider upgrading your plan
   here: julius.ai/settings/subscription

Q: How do credits work on annual plans?
A: Annual plan subscribers receive all their credits upfront when their subscription
   starts or renews. For example, a Pro annual subscriber gets all 50,000 credits for the
   year on day one. Your billing cycle and pricing stay the same.
   [NOTE: this "50,000" figure for Pro's annual total is itself inconsistent with the
   "Credits by plan" table (Pro = 5,000/month x 12 = 60,000/year) and with the pricing
   page ("60,000 credits per year" stated directly on the Pro card). Not coded against
   any variable for this record since Pro is not the entry tier, but noted here as
   corroborating evidence that this vendor's credit figures are internally inconsistent
   across its own documentation.]

Q: What does this change mean for my plan?
A: For most users, nothing changes except that Julius gets a lot better. Based on our
   data, fewer than 5% of users will exceed their monthly credit limits under normal
   usage. Most will see the same or more capacity than before, paired with noticeably
   better results.

Q: Do I need to do anything?
A: No action required. Existing paid users will be automatically moved to the
   credit-based system before their next billing cycle. Your plan and price stay exactly
   the same.

Q: What is the credit refund policy?
A: Credits are non-refundable, whether you bought them as a top-up or received them
   through your subscription. Credits are also non-transferable between accounts and are
   forfeited at the end of your billing period if your subscription is cancelled. This
   applies unless otherwise required by law. For full details, see the Refund Policy.

Q: Can I transfer credits to another account?
A: No. Credits are non-transferable and are tied to the account they were granted to.
