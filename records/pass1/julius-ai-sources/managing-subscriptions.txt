Managing Your Account / Managing Your Plan
Manage your Julius subscription: change plan, update payment methods, cancel, and manage
team workspaces.
Julius offers a Free plan as well as several paid plans. You can compare plans and see
current prices at julius.ai/pricing.

Upgrading Your Subscription
Navigate to the Subscription page. Click Upgrade under the plan you wish to upgrade to.
Complete your purchase on the Stripe checkout screen.
Upgrades take effect immediately, and your billing cycle resets to the upgrade date. If
you have credits remaining from your previous plan, they stack with your new plan's
credits. Existing credits keep their original expiration date — the end of the billing
period in which they were granted.

Downgrading Your Subscription
Navigate to the Subscription page. Click Downgrade under the plan you wish to switch to.
Confirm the change.
Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. You'll keep access to
your current plan's features and credits until then, and your new plan begins on your
next billing date. Any unused portion of your current plan is automatically applied as a
credit toward your next invoice — no separate refund is issued.

Switching Between Monthly and Annual Billing
You can switch your billing cadence from the Subscription page.
Monthly to Annual: Takes effect immediately. Your billing cycle resets to the switch
date.
Annual to Monthly: Takes effect at the end of your current annual billing period.
Need to switch from annual to monthly immediately? Reach out to team@julius.ai and we'll
process it for you.

Canceling Your Subscription
Navigate to the Subscription page. Click Invoices and Payment Methods to open your
billing portal. Click Cancel plan.
If eligible, you can select End subscription immediately and receive a refund.
After canceling, your plan stays active until the end of your current billing period.
For information on refunds, see our Refund Policy.
Subscribed through the App Store or Google Play? You must cancel through Apple or Google
directly — canceling on the website won't stop those charges. See our full cancel guide
for step-by-step instructions.

Canceling a Team Workspace
If you have a Business or Enterprise plan with a team workspace and want to remove it:
Navigate to the Subscription page. Navigate to the Teams tab. Scroll down to the Delete
Team section.

Managing Payment Methods and Billing Information
Navigate to the Subscription page. Click Invoices & Payment Methods. Click Add payment
method to change your current payment method. Click Update information to update your
billing information (e.g. name, address, company, VAT info).
