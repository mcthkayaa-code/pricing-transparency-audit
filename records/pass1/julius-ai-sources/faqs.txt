Q: What are the limitations on the row count/size of the data?
A: There is no limit enforced by us, but you may find a practical limit based on what can
   fit into 32G of RAM on the sandbox. If you are careful to write things to files and
   only read what is necessary then many users have used much larger datasets than this.
   You can also connect Julius to BigQuery or other data warehouses if you have large
   datasets.

Q: What are the limitations on the number of joins across data tables?
A: None!

Q: What is the refund policy?
A: Refunds are not automatically granted and are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. If you
   believe you are entitled to a refund, please contact us at team@julius.ai with your
   full name, the email address used for the purchase, your receipt number, and the
   reason for your request. Credits are non-refundable and have no cash value. Unused
   credits expire at the end of each billing cycle and do not carry over. For full
   details, view our Refund Policy.

Q: How do credits work?
A: Every action in Julius costs credits. The cost depends on what you're doing, which AI
   model is used, and how complex the task is. For a full breakdown, see Understanding
   credits.

Q: What can I do if my container is crashing?
A: If you're experiencing issues with your task, we recommend creating a new task. This
   resets the accumulated context and can help resolve most performance or
   functionality-related issues. If the issue persists after creating a new task, please
   reach out to our support team at team@julius.ai for further assistance.

Q: Julius crashed in the middle of an analysis
A: AI will sometimes do something called 'hallucinating' when it starts to go into loops
   and can't produce an output. Try to reword the prompt to be more specific and have
   more detail to help guide Julius. If there have been multiple crashes, it's best to
   start a new thread from scratch so the AI can refresh and restart to provide a better
   output.
