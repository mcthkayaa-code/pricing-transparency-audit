## Currency (resolves the D-003/D-007 currency check outright — vendor's own disclosure)
"All Fees are quoted in United States Dollar (USD) unless otherwise specified by Pixlr."
This is the strongest arm in the currency-check hierarchy (vendor's own currency
disclosure). No currency selector, country picker, or non-USD figure was observed anywhere
in official documents. Money values coded as plain USD figures, not `non_usd`.

## Auto-renewal
"The subscription to the Paid Services is based on an auto-renewal basis whereby it will
automatically be renewed upon the end of the application subscription period (i.e. monthly
basis or annual basis) corresponding to the term of Your subscription."
-> auto_renewal_default = on

Adjacent (not identical) clause, about price/package changes, not renewal-charge notice
specifically: "We reserve the right, in our sole discretion, to change, amend, modify or
vary the price, package and/or features of the Paid Services subscription plan(s), at any
time, with or without notice to You." No clause specifically committing to (or disclaiming)
advance notice of a renewal CHARGE was found -> renewal_notice_commitment = unknown
(the adjacent clause is about plan/price changes, not about renewal-charge notice; coding
`no_notice_stated` off it would be over-reading a different clause).

## Credits — rollover/expiry
"Any unused Credits at the end of the relevant month shall automatically expire and will
not roll over, accumulate, or be carried forward to any subsequent month. No refunds,
compensation, or replacement will be provided for any expired or unused Credits under the
subscription plan." Separately, "Purchased Credits" (a top-up product, distinct from plan
credits) are "valid for a period of one (1) year from the date of purchase and will expire
automatically after that period. Any unused Purchased Credits are also non-refundable."
-> credit_rollover_policy = expires_at_period_end (for the plan/subscription credits that
are the subject of this record; the separate one-year Credit-Pack expiry is noted here for
completeness, not coded as a second value — the codebook has one rollover variable).

## Fair-use / discretionary usage clause (F2)
"[AI generation, and AI-assisted editing tools] (collectively, "AI Services"), are provided
on a best-effort basis. The availability, performance, and quality of AI Services are not
guaranteed and may be modified, limited, or discontinued at any time, with or without
notice, at Pixlr's sole discretion. AI Services may be subject to fair-use limits, including
but not limited to rate limits, daily usage caps, and cooldown periods, to ensure equitable
access for all users. Pixlr reserves the right to adjust these limits at any time. Features
described as "unlimited" in any subscription plan are subject to fair-use policies and
reasonable usage limits."
-> unquantified_limit_clause = present (no figures given for the fair-use limits; explicit
discretionary-adjustment right reserved). This clause also settles that Plus's "Unlimited
saves" feature is qualified (not quantified) since it applies to "any subscription plan".

the AI Services based on the AI Input... ("AI Output"). AI Input and AI Output are
collectively referred to as "AI Content". When You use the AI Services, You grant Us a
non-exclusive, sublicensable, worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free and irrevocable right to
reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense and

Our discretion... Subject to the Agreements, You may use AI Content for personal and
commercial purposes."
-> commercial_use_lowest_tier: this grant is NOT stated as conditional on any paid tier.
Coded `free` on the text as written (see coder_note in the record for the residual

Restriction (same document): "You shall NOT: ... represent that AI Output is

## Output ownership — NOT explicitly stated either direction
Extensive rights/license language exists (above), but neither document contains an
affirmative ownership/title statement about AI-generated output specifically ("you own
the output" / "we own the output"). The one explicit ownership/title clause found in the
Terms of Use is scoped to the SERVICE/PLATFORM, not user output: "You are obtaining only a
limited license to use and access the Service... and that no ownership rights are being
conveyed to You. Pixlr retains all right, title and interest (including all patent,
copyright, trademark, trade secret and other intellectual property rights) in and to the
Services, all related and underlying technology and documentation..." — this is standard
platform/software-ownership boilerplate, not an output-ownership statement, and is NOT used
as evidence for `output_ownership_statement`.
-> output_ownership_statement = unknown (license/rights language is extensive; a
TITLE/OWNERSHIP statement about generated output specifically was not found)

## Watermarks
No mention in either document (`grep -i watermark` = zero hits in both ToS and License
Agreement plain text). The only "no watermarks" statements found anywhere on the site are
for unrelated free utility tools (JPG/PNG/PDF file converters) and a generic privacy-themed
line ("Your images are processed securely, and no watermarks are added to your files")
whose scope (which product/tier) is not tied to the free editor plan specifically — not used
as evidence for free_plan_watermark or watermark_removal_tier; both coded `unknown`.
