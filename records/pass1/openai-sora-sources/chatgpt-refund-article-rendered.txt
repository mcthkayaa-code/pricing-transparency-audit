How do I request a refund for my ChatGPT subscription?
Refund eligibility and request steps for ChatGPT subscriptions

How to request a refund
The process to request a refund for your subscription depends on how you initially signed up. To help us verify your account and process your request quickly, please contact us while logged in to the OpenAI account tied to the subscription or from the email address associated with the relevant charge.

You subscribed on the web at chatgpt.com
[self-serve chat-widget refund request flow described]
Our team will reply back to you as soon as possible. If you are eligible for a refund, we will process it within 5-7 business days. Accidental purchases are generally eligible for a refund if you contact us within 14 days of the charge.

You subscribed via Apple Subscriptions
For subscriptions made through Apple Subscriptions, cancellations and refunds must be requested directly from Apple.

You subscribed via Google Play Store
[same chat-widget flow] If you are eligible for a refund, we will process it within 10 business days. Accidental purchases are generally eligible for a refund if you contact us within 14 days of the charge.

Note for EU, UK, and Turkey residents
"If you live in the EU, UK, or Turkey, you are eligible for a prorated refund if you cancel your subscription within 14 days of purchase."

Note for Korea residents
"If you live in the Republic of Korea, you will receive a full refund if you request a refund within 7 days of the date of your purchase and have not used our service during that time. Otherwise, you will receive a pro-rated refund based on the period from the date you requested cancellation to the end of the subscription period you have paid for."

Once you request a refund, the cancellation of your subscription will take effect immediately, and you will no longer have access to the subscription service.
