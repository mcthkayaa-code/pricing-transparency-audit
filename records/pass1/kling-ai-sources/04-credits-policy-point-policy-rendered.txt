1. What are "Credits"?
"Credits" are a virtual currency designed to meet the needs of users who want to access more KLlNG AI services.
"Credits" can be used to redeem access to specific features or value-added services within the KLlNG AI platform.
"Credits" can only be obtained and used by logged-in users within the KLlNG AI platform.
For the avoidance of doubt, the Credits service is a network technology and related service provided by the platform to users. The Credits service is not an online payment service, and Credits are not tokens, virtual currencies, or prepaid vouchers. They do not have monetary value or prepaid value.

4. What is the Usage Order of "Credits"?
The usage order is based on the "Credits Validity Period". Credits with a shorter validity period will be used first
5.  What can "Credits" be used for and how are they used?
Currently, after users purchase Credits, they can use AI creation through the consumption of "Credits" on the Kling AI platform and our partner's platforms. For more information, please refer to the related interfaces.
6. What is the relation between Credits and Membership?
Credits can only be used for access specific features and services on the Kling AI platform and partner's platforms.
Credits cannot be exchanged for value-added services such as  services for Membership. Credits cannot be transferred or gifted between different Users, between different accounts controlled by the same User, or between different spaces owned by the same User (e.g., personal, team, or enterprise spaces). For Team Memberships, the Team Owner and Administrators may manage Credits within the Team Space, including recharging and allocating Credits. Credits are non-refundable and cannot be redeemed for cash; in addition, some features (e.g., Fast-track generation, 1080p videos, Watermark removal, Master shot & video extension, Image upscaling) are exclusively available to Membership and cannot be accessed through Credits.
