FAQ

What size files can Anomaly AI analyze?

Anomaly AI supports Excel and CSV uploads up to 1GB. For datasets larger than that, or datasets that update regularly, connect a warehouse or database such as BigQuery, Snowflake, or MySQL.

Is this only for Excel files?

No. Excel and CSV are the easiest starting points for large-file workflows, but Anomaly AI also supports connected data sources such as BigQuery, Snowflake, GA4, Google Sheets, and MySQL.

How is this different from uploading a file to a chatbot?

Chatbots can be useful for small one-off files, but they hit context and repeatability limits on larger datasets. Anomaly AI is built for recurring business reports: structured analysis, dashboards, refreshable workflows, and logic you can inspect.

Can I use AI for recurring large dataset reports?

Yes. The strongest use case is repeatable reporting: connect the source once, ask for the dashboard or report you need, then schedule recurring email reports when the workflow repeats.

Does Anomaly AI do real-time monitoring?

Anomaly AI supports dashboards, scheduled reports, and proactive reporting workflows. It should not be described as a live streaming or real-time anomaly monitoring system.
