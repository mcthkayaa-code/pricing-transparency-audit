How to Use Credits on HeyGen

This article explains how credits work on HeyGen’s credit-based plans, how credits apply to different features, and how to get more credits if you run out.

Written by Avi
Updated this week

🛎️ This article covers HeyGen’s newer credit-based plans. If you are on a legacy unlimited plan, your plan may still use the older Premium Credit Pack system. Legacy users can keep their existing plan as long as their subscription remains active and in good standing, but if you cancel your legacy plan or switch to a credit-based plan, you will not be able to switch back to the old legacy plan.

What are credits?

Credits are the usage system for HeyGen's credit-based paid plans. The updated plans come with significantly more credits than before. Creator now includes 3x the monthly credits than before, giving you more room to use HeyGen's latest and most advanced models. Rather than splitting usage between unlimited features and a smaller Premium Credit pool, credits work as a single balance across supported video generation and asset creation features, including Avatar IV/V and other cutting-edge capabilities. This makes it straightforward to understand what your plan includes and how much each feature costs.

Credits can be used for supported HeyGen features like Avatar III, Avatar IV / V, Video Agent, video translation, B-roll generation, Generate Looks, and more.

How credits work on each plan

Creator

Creator includes 600 credits per month.

If you run out of credits on Creator, you can upgrade to Pro for more credits. Creator does not support one-time credit purchases.

Pro

Pro starts at 1,000 credits per month and can scale up to higher credit tiers depending on your needs.

If you run out of credits on Pro, you can upgrade to a higher Pro tier with more monthly credits. Pro does not use one-time credit packs. Instead, you can choose the Pro tier that best matches your expected usage.

Business

Business includes 1,500 credits per month.

If you run out of credits on Business, you can purchase additional credits or turn on auto-reload.

Additional Business credits cost $0.05 per credit and are purchased in blocks of 100 credits for $5. You can use the + and - buttons in the add-ons flow to increase or decrease the number of credit blocks before checkout.

How credits apply to each feature

Different features use credits at different rates. Some features are charged per minute of generated output, while others use a fixed number of credits per generation.

How can I purchase more credits?

Creator users

Creator users cannot purchase one-time credit packs.

If you need more credits, you can upgrade to Pro from your Plan & Billing settings.

Pro users

Pro users can get more credits by upgrading to a higher Pro tier.

To upgrade:

  • Log in to your HeyGen account.

  • Go to Settings on the bottom left.

  • Open Plan & Billing.

  • Choose a higher Pro tier with the credit amount that matches your needs.

  • Complete checkout.

When you upgrade mid-cycle, you’ll be charged the prorated difference and receive the additional credits right away. Your full new credit allocation will apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

Business users

Business users can purchase one-time-packs that are not subscription add-ons and are purchased once to recharge your account with credits.

To purchase additional Business credits:

  • Log in to your HeyGen account.

  • Go to Settings on the bottom left.

  • Open Plan & Billing.

  • Go to Manage Add-ons.

  • Select the additional credit option.

  • Use the + and - buttons to choose how many 100-credit blocks you want to purchase.

  • Complete checkout.

Business credit one-time-pack

  • Minimum purchase: $5 for 100 credits

  • Each additional block: +$5 for +100 credits

  • Cost per credit: $0.05

  • Optional auto-reload is available

Auto-reload can help avoid interruptions by automatically adding credits when your balance runs low.

Do credits roll over?

Yes, depending on your billing type.

Monthly subscribers:

  • Unused monthly plan credits roll over for one additional billing cycle, as long as the user stays on a paid plan.

    In practice, credits have up to 60 days before they expire: the original 30-day subscription cycle, plus the next 30-day cycle.

  • If you cancel your paid plan and move to Free, unused credits do not roll over.

Annual subscribers:

  • Annual users receive their credits in monthly allocations, but they have the full annual subscription period to use them.

  • Unused credits accumulate until your annual renewal date.

  • If a user has an annual Pro 1000 plan, they receive 1,000 credits each month.

    • Jan 1: User receives 1,000 credits

    • Feb 1: Another 1,000 credits are added

    • Mar 1: Another 1,000 credits are added

  • If a user does not use any credits, the balance continues accumulating during the annual cycle.

  • If you cancel your paid plan and move to Free, unused credits do not roll over.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Free users have credits?

No. Free users do not use credits. Free users can create 1–3 free videos depending on their region.

Can I buy credits on Creator?

No. Creator does not support one-time credit purchases. If you need more credits, you can upgrade to Pro.

Can I buy credits on Pro?

Pro users can get more credits by upgrading to a higher Pro tier. Pro does not use one-time credit packs on the new credit-based system.

Can I buy credits on Business?

Yes. Business users can buy additional credits in 100-credit blocks. The minimum purchase is $5 for 100 credits, and each additional block adds another 100 credits for $5.

Can I turn on auto-reload?

Auto-reload is available for Business users. This helps automatically add credits when your balance runs low.

What happens if I upgrade mid-cycle?

If you upgrade mid-cycle, you’ll be charged the prorated difference and receive the additional credits right away. Your full new credit allocation will apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

What happens if I cancel my plan?

If you cancel your paid plan and move to Free, unused credits do not roll over. You’ll keep access to your paid plan until the end of your current billing cycle.

Can I switch back to my old legacy unlimited plan?

No. If you cancel your legacy unlimited plan or switch to a credit-based plan, you will not be able to switch back to the old legacy plan.

Do credit-based plans still have priority processing limits?

No. Priority processing quotas do not apply to credit-based plans.

However, users may still see videos in “pending” because of concurrency limits. For example, the system will not process 20 videos at the same time.

Generation time still depends on the video type, length, and system capacity. You can see more about concurrency limits here.

For users still on unlimited plans, the existing priority quota still applies based on their plan.
