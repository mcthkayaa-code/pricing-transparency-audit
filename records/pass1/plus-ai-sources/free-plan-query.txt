# does plus ai have a free plan separate from the free trial

No — **Plus AI doesn’t have a free plan separate from the free trial**.

* **To use Plus AI, you need a paid plan.** New customers can start a **7-day free trial** (requires a credit card; you can cancel anytime during the trial). After the trial, you’re charged for the plan you selected.\
  See [Plans and billing](/plus-ai/plans-and-billing.md).
* There *is* a **free plan**, but it’s for **Snapshots** (different feature), with limits like **5 Snapshots per organization**.\
  See [Free and paid plans](/other/live-snapshots/free-and-paid-plans.md).
