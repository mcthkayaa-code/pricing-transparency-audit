# is an annual plan charged as one lump sum payment for the full 12 months upfront, or billed monthly during a 12-month term?

An **annual** Plus subscription is **billed on an annual cadence**, not as monthly charges spread across the 12-month term.

* Paid plans are “billed either **monthly or annually**” depending on what you choose.
* Subscriptions “automatically renew every month or **every year**.”
* When switching **from an annual plan to a monthly plan**, your account gets a **credit for the payment you already made for your annual plan**, which is then applied to your future monthly payments.

So, for an annual plan, you’re effectively charged for the full annual period at renewal time (rather than being billed month-by-month during the year). See [Plans and billing](/plus-ai/plans-and-billing.md).
