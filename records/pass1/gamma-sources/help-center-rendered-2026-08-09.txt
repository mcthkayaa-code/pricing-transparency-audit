Introduction

If you've recently subscribed to Gamma and had a change of plans, we've made it simple to
request a refund - if you meet the eligibility criteria. This article outlines Gamma's refund
policy and how to contact support for a refund.

What is Gamma's Refund Policy?

Important: Beginning December 1, 2025, Gamma will offer refunds only within the first 3 days of
subscribing to one of our paid plans, for both monthly and annual billing options, and only upon
contacting Customer Support. You will not automatically receive a refund if you cancel within 3
days of subscribing. Additionally, you must not have exceeded the threshold for credit usage.

Annual and Monthly Plans

Eligible for a full refund within the first 3 days after subscribing, if credit usage does not
exceed the threshold for the plan. See the table below to understand the credit usage threshold
per plan.

No refunds are issued after 3 days, except where required by applicable law.

Refund eligibility conditions, including credit usage thresholds, are subject to applicable
local consumer protection laws.

Plan | Monthly Credits | Threshold | Refund available if requested within 3 days of subscribing and
Plus | 1k | 20% | less than 200 credits used
Pro | 4k | 15% | less than 600 credits used
Team | 6k | 15% | less than 900 credits used
Business | 10k | 10% | less than 1,000 credits used
Ultra | 20k | 10% | less than 2,000 credits used

Note: To receive your refund, you must request one within the 3-day window. Our team will
review your eligibility.

Cancellation Policy & Renewal Timeline

To avoid being charged for the next billing period, cancel at least 30 days before your plan's
renewal date. If you received an auto-renewal notification, you have 30 days from the date of
that notice to cancel. Your subscription will remain active until the current term ends.

Note: Cancelling your subscription does not automatically issue a refund. If you subscribed
within the last 3 days and haven't exceeded your plan's credit usage threshold, you may be
eligible for a refund - let us know and we'll check for you.

To cancel: Settings (gear icon) > Billing > "Cancel subscription" at the bottom of the Billing
page. Self-serve, no ticket/email/call required. Subscription remains active until the end of
the current billing period.
