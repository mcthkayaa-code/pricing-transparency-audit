Q: Can customers make commercial use of the images generated with AI tools powered by Firefly?
A: In general, you may use outputs from generative AI features commercially. However, if Adobe designates in the product or elsewhere that a beta version of a generative AI feature cannot be used commercially, then the generated outputs from that beta feature are for personal use only and cannot be used commercially.

Q: What does "designed to be commercially safe" mean for Adobe Firefly in Adobe Express?
A: It means that Adobe is taking a thoughtful approach to how the Firefly family of models is being developed, including the sources of content used for training in accordance with Adobe's AI Ethics Principles.
