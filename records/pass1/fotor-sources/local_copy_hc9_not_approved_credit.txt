Title: Why is the AI generated image "not approved"? What are the offensive words? Does it consume credit? – Fotor Help Center
Fotor Help Center > AI Tools > AIGC (AI Image Generator)
Nora, 3 years ago Updated

Why is the image "not approved"? Two main reasons: (1) Offensive language in the prompt is automatically banned, the image is not generated and an "Image not approved" message is shown. (2) Offensive content: the generated image itself may be flagged by the NSFW filter before being displayed, even if the prompt had no offensive words; false positives are acknowledged as possible.

What offensive words can be automatically banned? Explicit sexual language, racial slurs, hate speech, and other offensive/discriminatory language; the banned-word list is updated frequently.

Does it consume credit when the generated image is not approved?
No, your credits will not be consumed when a generated image is not approved. You will only be charged credits when an image is generated and approved successfully.
