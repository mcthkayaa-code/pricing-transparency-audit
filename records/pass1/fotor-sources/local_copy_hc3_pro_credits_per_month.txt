Title: How many credits do Pro members have per month? Do the credits stack up? – Fotor Help Center
Nora, 6 months ago Updated

Members who subscribe to the Pro plan receive a monthly credits of 100, whereas members who opt for the Pro+ plan receive 300 credits every month. The Max package includes 1000 credits per month.

Please note that for the Pro/Pro+/Max annual plans, points are still issued on a monthly basis, not all at once for the year.

Unused credits are carried forward to the following month, but can be accumulated for a maximum period of five months.
