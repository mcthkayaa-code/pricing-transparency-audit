Free AI Visibility Tools

A small set of free tools for getting your brand into AI answers — from making sure the crawlers can even reach you, to seeing whether they mention you yet. No signup, nothing stored. Each one is built around the same honest idea: tell you exactly where you stand, not where we wish you stood.

AI Bot Checker

Enter your domain to see whether your robots.txt blocks GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and other AI crawlers — the most common silent reason a brand never shows up in AI answers.

AI Readiness Checker

Paste a page URL and get a 0–100 score for how ready it is to be cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews — crawler access, freshness, schema, author and extractable answers, each checked live.

AI Prompt Generator

Generate the real buyer prompts people type into ChatGPT and Perplexity for your category, so you know exactly which answers to check yourself against.

llms.txt Generator

Build an llms.txt file that points AI assistants at the pages on your site that matter most. Copy, paste, publish at your root.
