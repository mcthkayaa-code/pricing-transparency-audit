Hi, I'm Minel Gunesoglu
Founder of Is My Brand in AI — I help brands get named when people ask AI.
I came to AI search from the field that has always been about one thing: getting noticed. I studied Advertising, alongside Public Relations, at Akdeniz University, graduating in 2017. I'd started in tourism a year earlier, in 2016, and spent a decade there — most of it in customer relations (guest experience, in hospitality terms) at well-known hospitality brands like The Land of Legends, Rixos Hotels, and Nirvana Hotels, leaving as an Assistant Customer Relations Manager. Ten years of making sure customers chose us, and the brand got remembered, taught me that visibility is the whole game.
And the rules of visibility just changed. The question is no longer “do we rank on Google” — it is “does the AI name us when someone asks.” So I pivoted the same craft — advertising, attention, reputation — to the AI era. Today I build Is My Brand in AI full-time and in public, and I test every tactic on my own pages before I recommend it.
