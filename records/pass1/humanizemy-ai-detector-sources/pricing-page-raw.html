Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about HumanizeMyAI.

What is an AI humanizer?
An AI humanizer rewrites AI-assisted drafts so they read more naturally. HumanizeMyAI draws on a documented corpus of 2,590 real long-form essays, then runs each rewrite past its own pattern check before handing back the result. The goal is clearer, less repetitive prose without a generic paraphrased feel.
Can results vary across AI-detection tools?
Yes. AI detectors use different models and can return different results for the same passage. HumanizeMyAI focuses on naturalness, clarity, and structural variety; it does not guarantee any detector score. We publish dated internal evaluations so customers can review the methodology and limits.
What is the 2,590-essay corpus?
It's a curated dataset of 2,590 permissioned long-form English essays — over five million words — that grounds how the humanizer writes. This is the difference between us and a black-box humanizer: our style source is named, sized, and documented.
What patterns does your AI detector check?
The pattern scanner names the tells a reader would notice: repeated transition phrases, three-verb parallel lists, uniform sentence-length variance, hedged-claim density, scholar-attribution chains, and roughly two dozen more. Typographic habits such as em-dash frequency are deliberately not scored, because we measured them at chance. The free /detect page adds a trained model on top, which is what produces the verdict there.
Do you take affiliate commissions from competitors?
No. We mention competitors (WriteHuman, QuillBot, StealthGPT, Duey, Grammarly) by name throughout the site for honest comparison, and we don't earn a cent if you click through and sign up with them. Most comparison sites in this category run on affiliate revenue — which means their rankings are paid placements. Ours aren't.
Is the free tier really free?
Yes — 4 humanizations per day, up to 125 words per run, no signup, no credit card. The free quota resets at midnight UTC. We also cap by IP at 50 humanizations per day so the free tier survives. If you need higher limits or longer documents, paid plans start at $12/mo (yearly) or $18/mo (monthly).
Does HumanizeMyAI support languages other than English?
Not yet. The humanizer is optimized for English — academic, business, and creative English prose specifically. Output on other languages may technically work, but we haven't tuned the corpus or the prompt for them, so quality is unpredictable. Spanish, German, and French are on the roadmap once English ranks stably.
Who is HumanizeMyAI for?
HumanizeMyAI is built for content marketers, SEO professionals, freelance writers, publishers, agencies, and non-native English professionals who refine their own AI-assisted drafts. It is not a ghostwriting service and is not designed to fabricate documents or misrepresent authorship.
