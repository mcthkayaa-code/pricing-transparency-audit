Can I get a refund after subscribing?
Jason
August 23, 2023 08:06
Updated
You can cancel your subscription at any time to stop the auto-renewal of your subscription.
If you decide to cancel your subscription, you will still have access to your subscription until the day of expiration and will be charged accordingly. You can also reactivate the auto-renewal of your subscription anytime before the subscription expires.
Once your subscription expires, your account will turn into a free plan account that can be used indefinitely.
