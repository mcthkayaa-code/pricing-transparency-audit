Major Faults: If a product or service has a major fault, such as not functioning as described, or being significantly defective.
Misleading Description: If the product or service is not as described.

Technical Issues: If persistent technical issues prevent access to the purchased product or service.
Non-Delivery of Tokens: If tokens purchased are not delivered or credited to your account due to system errors.
Defective Digital Goods: If purchased digital goods are significantly defective or not as described.

You misused the product or service in a way that contributed to the problem;
