Method: WebFetch (HTML->markdown extraction; not Cloudflare-blocked, this subdomain served directly)
---
Token cost table (per generation):
- Default Image (768x768): 1 Token (single) / 4 Tokens (batch)
- Canvas: 1 Token per image
- Image guidance (free): 1 Token per image
- Elements: 1 Token (one) / 4 Tokens (four images)
- Prompt Magic V2: 2 Tokens (single) / 8 Tokens (batch)
- Remove Background: 5 Tokens (single only)
- Upscalers: 5 Tokens (single only)
- Unzoom V1: 5 Tokens (single only)
- Alchemy (PMv3 OFF): 8 Tokens (single) / 18 Tokens (batch)
- Alchemy V2 (Lightning XL models): 10 Tokens (single) / 12 Tokens (batch)
- Full 3D Texture Generation: 30 Tokens (single only)
"Free users have a daily allowance of 150 Tokens" that resets every 24 hours. Paid plan users receive monthly allowances with reset cycles.
No explicit definition sentence of "one token equals X" as a standalone statement, but the rate table itself ties 1 Token to 1 Default Image (768x768) generation -- this is the operative conversion for the principal output (image).

ARTICLE: Token Rollover
URL: https://intercom.help/leonardo-ai/en/articles/9560707-token-rollover
Method: WebFetch (HTML->markdown extraction)
---
"The Rollover Bank accumulates up to 3 months worth of tokens."
Rollover bank caps by plan: Essential 25,500 / Premium 75,000 / Ultimate 180,000 -- matches exactly the "Token Bank" figures shown on the pricing page (3x the monthly Fast Token allowance in each case: 8,500x3=25,500; 25,000x3=75,000; 60,000x3=180,000).
"Rollover tokens will only be used once all Fast tokens have been used."
"The Rollover Bank only stores tokens on paid plans with an active subscription."
"If you cancel your Premium plan, any unused tokens in your Rollover bank will be cancelled, so it's important to keep your plan active, or use your saved tokens before cancelling your plan."
Downgrades: "Any tokens above this limit will expire."

Full verbatim relevant text:
"Can I use my image generations for commercial purposes? The good news is that as per our Terms of Service, you can indeed use your images for commercial purposes! There are a few caveats though..."
"Leonardo.Ai allows you to use the website for both personal and commercial purposes, however, the content generated by users is subject to and governed by our Terms of Service."
"Effectively, when generating privately using a paid subscription, users retain full ownership, copyright, and all other intellectual property rights to their images."
"For images that are made public, Leonardo.Ai and its successors retain worldwide perpetual rights to use, copy, reproduce, process, adapt, modify, publish, transmit, create derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute images and text prompts you input into the services, or any assets produced by the service at your direction."
"Additionally, other users are granted a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to access these publicly available assets through the service..."
Example 1 (Jason, FREE plan user, T-shirt business): "Jason can use free tools such as the AI Generations tool to create new concepts... However, all of Jason's design concepts will also be publicly viewable for other Leonardo users to copy and remix..."
Example 2 (Jane, PAID plan user): "Jane's design concepts have the option to remain private, preventing other Leonardo users from viewing and making their own variations."
Conclusion: "In both examples, Jason and Jane can download their own generations for commercial usage. However, Jason might also have his designs used by others too, due to his images being public. This is the main distinction."
=> Commercial use is explicitly confirmed for BOTH free and paid tiers; the difference is public (free) vs. optionally-private (paid) content, and ownership (paid=user owns; free=Leonardo owns per ToS 8.7, but user still may use commercially per this article).
