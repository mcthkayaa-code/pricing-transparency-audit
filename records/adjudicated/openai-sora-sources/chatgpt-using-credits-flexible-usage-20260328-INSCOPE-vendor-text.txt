Credits are a pay-as-you-go add-on for Codex & Sora when you need more usage beyond plan limits How credits work Your plan’s included usage is used first . After you hit plan limits, usage draws from your credit balance . How to buy credits Codex If you hit a usage limit in Codex, you’ll see a banner with “Add credits.” You can also buy credits from Codex Settings → Usage → Credits on both web and the Codex app. Eligible Plus and Pro users can also turn on Auto top-up from Codex Settings → Usage . When your credit balance drops below your selected minimum balance, we automatically purchase only the amount needed to return it to your selected target balance using your default payment method on file. If your balance is already below the minimum when you turn it on, a purchase may happen right away. Sora App: If you hit usage limit in Sora, you’ll see an option in the Sora App to buy more video gens. You can also buy more video gens anytime in the Sora Web from Settings -> Usage Note: Auto top-up applies to your shared credits balance, so those credits can still be used across supported features like Codex and Sora. There is not a separate Sora-only auto top-up setting. Your credits are flexible — they work across any supported feature available in your plan. Currently, this includes Codex for Plus/Pro subscribers and Sora for all users. For example, if you buy credits in Codex, you can use those credits for Sora generations in the Sora App too. You choose where to use them. Supported features are subject to change over time. Can I use auto top-up with credits? Eligible Plus and Pro users can turn on auto top-up from Codex Settings → Usage Dashboard. When your credit balance drops below your selected minimum balance, we automatically purchase only the amount needed to return it to your selected target balance using your default payment method on file. If your balance is already below the minimum when you turn it on, a purchase may happen right away. Auto top-up applies to your shared credits balance, so those credits can still be used across supported features like Codex and Sora. There is not a separate Sora-only auto top-up setting.
