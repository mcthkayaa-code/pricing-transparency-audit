 All Collections Sora Sora - Release Notes Sora - Release Notes Updated: 6 days ago March 19, 2026 Edit videos in Sora Today, we’re introducing an editor in Sora on iOS and web, with Android coming soon. The editor lets you refine and build on your videos in one place, so you can move from generation to editing to iteration without leaving Sora. With the editor, you can trim clips with frame-level precision, stitch multiple clips into a single sequence, reorder clips in your timeline, and import videos directly from your drafts. You can also extend a clip forward, reprompt a segment, or remix an existing clip to create new variations without starting over. February 9, 2026 Continue your story with Extensions Now you can seamlessly continue any video in Sora with Extensions . Open an existing draft, tap Extend , and describe what should happen next. Sora will carry your scene forward—preserving your characters, settings, and overall vibe—while bringing your next chapter to life. Your extended scene appears as a new, longer draft, so you can keep building without losing earlier versions. Whether you’re expanding a single moment or developing a full narrative arc, Extensions make it easy to create richer, more immersive stories with consistent worlds and characters. Rolling out today on the Sora iOS app and Sora.com to all users, everywhere Sora is available. February 4, 2026 Image 2 Video with people As we continue to strengthen Sora’s guardrails, we’re enabling more creative expression and connection, including letting people create videos from photos of family and friends. Animating photos of family and friends is one of Sora’s most requested features. People use it to bring treasured memories to life and connect with loved ones. It is one of our most used features in ChatGPT Image Generation, so we understand users are eager to bring the magic of Sora to their photos as well.  Starting today, eligible users can upload images with people to make videos in Sora, after attesting that they have consent from people featured and rights to upload the media. Image-to-video generations with people are subject to particularly strict safety guardrails, even stricter than what’s allowed with Sora Characters (formerly known as the cameo feature). Images including kids and young-looking persons are subject to even stricter moderation and guardrails about what can be created from them. In addition, Image 2 Video generations where a realistic person is detected in the input image will be visually stylized automatically, for added clarity content is AI-generated. Videos will also have watermarks upon sharing as well. Sora continues to prohibit video generations of known public figures, even for image to video. We also continue to have multiple moderation, reporting and takedown measures in place. Misuse is subject to action under our policies .  Characters remain the best way to create highly realistic videos of someone’s audiovisual likeness with controls set by the individual. Characters also allow more creative freedom than what’s allowed via Image 2 Video.  During significant testing, we saw this feature used in many meaningful ways, which informed our goal to make it available more broadly, safely. We look forward to seeing more of what everyone creates.  December 18, 2025 Sora is now available  in Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Mexico, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, and Uruguay. November 24, 2025 Today we’re introducing video styles in Sora. We’re starting with six preset options—Thankful, Vintage, Comic, News, Musical, and Selfie—that help you create videos with distinct aesthetics and themes without needing to prompt for them directly. These styles help spark ideas and make it easier to bring ideas to life effectively and expressively. To use them, create a video by tapping ‘+’ and open the Styles tab next to Cameos, then write your prompt and choose the style you want. Your draft will be created with that style applied. Sora styles are rolling out today to all users across Sora.com and the Sora iOS app, wherever Sora is available. November 4, 2025 Today, we’re releasing the Sora app on Android in all markets where Sora is available (U.S., Canada, Japan, Korea, Thailand, Vietnam, Taiwan). At launch, features like creating characters, stitching, and the ability to purchase additional videos beyond included usage is not supported, but we’re working quickly on adding these. You can download the Sora app now on the Play Store. October 30, 2025 Today we’re introducing ways to purchase additional usage in products like Codex and Sora. If you hit your included limits, you can seamlessly buy more credits right in the Codex dashboard or buy more video generations in the Sora app to keep going. Learn more in our article: Using Credits for Flexible Usage in ChatGPT (Free/Go/Plus/Pro) & Sora. October 29, 2025 Today, we’re introducing character cameos in Sora. You’ve already been able to create videos of your own cameo—now you can create cameos of the characters in your life and imagination: your cat, a plushie, a doodle, or an original persona dreamt up in Sora. These characters can be tagged and reused in future generations, by you—and, if you choose, by others. Creating a character is easy. You can upload a video—from your camera roll, including downloaded Sora videos—or create one directly in the app by tapping ‘⋯’ on any video or draft and selecting ‘Create character.’ Once created, each character comes with its own permissions, separate from your personal likeness: keep it just for yourself, share it with mutual followers, or open it to everyone on Sora. Give your character a display name and handle, and tag it whenever you want it to appear in a video. We’re launching with starter pack characters for you to play with, including classic Halloween character cameos like: Frankenstein (@franklyfrankenstein) Dracula (@soradracula) Jack O Lantern (@sorajackolantern) Witch (@sorawitch) Ghost (@ghostlyghost) We can’t wait to see the videos—and characters—you bring to life. We’re also making it easier to create more expansive videos with stitching on the Sora app and sora.com. In drafts, tap ‘select’ and choose the clips you want to stitch together. Then, click ‘stitch’ to connect the videos. The stitched video will appear in your drafts once ready. Stitching allows you to create more sophisticated narratives and extend your story, with granular control over each segment. Finally, we’re introducing leaderboards in the Sora app, so you can instantly see the most remixed videos and the most cameoed users and characters—then jump right into what the community’s creating together. October 15, 2025 Today, we’re launching storyboards for Sora 2 in sora.com in beta. Storyboards let you sketch out your video second by second, making it easier to bring more detailed ideas to life. Available first to ChatGPT Pro users, you can access storyboards by selecting ‘storyboard’ in the composer on sora.com . From there, you can build your video frame by frame from scratch —just like storyboards in Sora 1—or simply describe a scene , choose a duration, and let Sora generate a detailed storyboard you can edit. We’re also introducing new duration options for longer videos—perfect for ideas that need a few extra beats to fully unfold. All users can generate 15-second videos on the app and web , in addition to the 10-second default. Additionally, Pro users can now generate videos of 25 seconds on web with storyboard. Usage: 15-second videos count as two videos toward daily limits; 25-second videos count as four. How to set duration: On the Sora app, tap the dropdown under “Create video” at the top of the compose screen and select “Duration.” On sora.com, click the settings icon at the bottom left of the composer bar and select “Duration.” Need more help?
