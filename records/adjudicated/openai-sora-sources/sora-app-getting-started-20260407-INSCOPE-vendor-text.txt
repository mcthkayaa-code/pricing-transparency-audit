 All Collections Sora Getting started with the Sora app Getting started with the Sora app Updated: 2 months ago Sora 2 is available on the Sora iOS app, the Sora 2 Android app, and on sora.com . We are slowly enabling access to the Sora app and Sora 2. You can request to be notified when you receive access in the Sora app . Sora is a new OpenAI app for creating short videos with synchronized audio. It’s powered by our next-generation model, Sora 2, which improves realism, physics, and instruction-following. The app is designed for low-friction, collaborative creation - make something from text or a photo, remix what you love, and share with friends. Sora uses your existing OpenAI account; sign in with the same credentials you use for ChatGPT. Download the Sora app on iOS and sign in with your OpenAI account. During onboarding you’ll be asked for your birthday to help set age-appropriate protections. In the current invite-only phase you may also need to enter an invite code. You can pick a username, add a profile photo (optional), and find friends to follow. What you can make Sora is best for short clips that feel real and responsive to direction. You can: Describe a scene to generate a 10-second vertical video (default 9:16) with synchronized audio. Start from an image to animate a still. Remix an existing post to create your own take, with clear remix labeling. Please note that Image to video with depictions of real people is not supported at the moment. To create a video, open the Sora app and tap + . Choose whether to start from scratch and describe a new video or upload an image . Describe what you want to see: subject, setting, motion, camera style, pacing, and the kind of audio you want (ambience, dialogue, sounds). The Sora 2 model follows detailed instructions closely; concise, specific directions lead to the most reliable results. Generate a preview, then iterate—tweak your prompt, apply a different style, or use Remix to branch without losing your original. When you’re happy, save the draft. You can publish immediately or later, either to everyone or just select people via direct messages. Please note that Sora 2 can make mistakes. It can struggle with scenes containing many people speaking at once, with complex collisions, and with very rapid camera moves. If you see mistakes, try shorter prompts, simpler motion, fewer characters, or more explicit camera instructions. Creating characters on Sora Characters let you cast yourself (or approved friends) in videos. Setting up a character is opt-in and includes a quick video-and-audio verification so Sora can represent you accurately and to prevent misuse. You control who can use your character on a person-by-person basis and can revoke access at any time. You can also remove videos that include your character, including drafts created by others. At launch, generating videos based on uploaded images that include real people is blocked. Learn more about generating content with characters. FAQ Who can use Sora today? Access starts with invite-only iOS users beginning September 30, 2025, then expands. You can download the app to be notified when your account is eligible. Where can I use Sora? iOS app at launch, sora.com on web with deeper controls, and Android later. Availability starts with U.S. and Canada and will then expand. The United Kingdom, EU, and Australia are not yet included at launch. Where can I use Sora 2 Pro? Sora 2 Pro can be accessed by ChatGPT Pro users on the web. As rollout progresses this will become available to users within the Sora app on Android & iOS. What’s the difference between Sora 2 and Sora 2 Pro? Sora 2 is tuned for speed and everyday creation. Sora 2 Pro targets higher fidelity and tougher shots and may take longer to generate. As rollout progresses, ChatGPT Pro and API users will gain access to Sora 2 Pro. Does Sora generate audio? Yes. Video and audio are created together. You can guide the generated sound in your prompt - for example, by providing dialogue or describing the audio. Can I import media? You can start from an image. At launch, image to video that depicts real people isn’t supported. We’ll share updates as other import options evolve. What about video-to-video? Transformation of existing full videos isn’t planned for launch. Use Remix to branch from posts you see in Sora. Can I control who uses my likeness? Yes. Characters are opt-in and permissioned. You can approve specific people, revoke access, and remove videos that include your character. You can also see any video that uses your character, including unpublished drafts created by others. Are videos watermarked? Yes. At launch, all Sora videos include a visible moving watermark. They also embed C2PA metadata—an industry-standard, tamper-proof signature. What’s happening to Sora 1? Sora 1 Turbo is available to use for the time being. Your previous creations stay in your sora.com library. However, the Sora 1 web experience is actively being deprecated, and customers should transition away from Sora 1 web and look forward to Sora for Business. Expect more details in the coming months. How is the feed ranked? “For you” is personalized to inspire creation. See more about the principles that guide our personalization here . You can also control whether Sora will use your ChatGPT memories to personalize recommendations in Settings, and parents can adjust personalization for their kids via ChatGPT’s parental controls . You can switch to Following, Top, or Latest, and filter by mood. Remixes and characters are clearly labeled.
