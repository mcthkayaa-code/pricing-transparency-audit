Characters aren’t always perfect today. Sometimes a voice may take on an unintended accent, or a body image might not look quite right. We’re actively working on advanced tools to help you better tune your character, and in the meantime you can use the character instructions field to guide Sora toward a closer match. We’ll keep improving this with your feedback. To start a new generation: Open the Sora app and tap + . Do one of the following: Describe your scene in the prompt window. Upload a still image as inspiration by selecting the image icon. Please note: uploading images with depictions of real people is blocked. Characters are the only way to use one’s likeness in Sora, which requires explicit permission from the likeness holder. 3. Write your prompt. You can add optional details, such as: Subject & setting: who/what, where, time of day. Camera & motion: “wide establishing shot,” “slow dolly left,” “quick cut to close-up.” Look & pacing: “iPhone-style realism,” “90s sitcom timing,” “dreamy bokeh.” Audio intent: ambience, sounds, dialogue cues (“two friends chatting in a café; clinking cups, soft jazz”). 4. (Optional) Select a character. Learn more about generating content with Characters. 5. (Optional) Select a style - your draft will be created with that style applied. 6. At the top of the screen, you can set the Orientation to either Portrait or Landscape. You can also set a duration of 10 or 15 seconds. 7. Press the ↑ to generate. You can see your progress by going to your drafts. Once the video is completed, you’ll get a push/in-app notification when it’s done. 8. To edit or regenerate your video, select Edit video. You can trim, reorder, stitch, extend, reprompt, or remix clips, or regenerate the video with the same prompt or an updated prompt. You can also select the ⋯ icon on the top-right of the screen to download your video, or delete it. 9. (Optional) Press the description of the video to update the caption before publishing. 10. Select Post video to publish your video to the Sora app feed, or to select people via direct messages. Videos sent via DMs won’t be visible on the global Sora feed. Create an image (web only) Image creation is available on sora.com (not in the iOS app at launch). Go to sora.com → Create → Image . Choose: Describe your scene in the prompt window. Upload a still image as inspiration by selecting the image icon. Pick a Style if you like and write a clear prompt. Generate and Download . Images can be used later as inputs for Image-to-Video (subject to people/likeness rules). Prompting tips Be specific, then iterate. Start with the core intent; add camera, motion, and pacing on the next pass. Describe timing. “Three-beat gag: wide → medium → close-up; quick cuts.” Anchor realism. “Overcast afternoon; handheld jitter; phone microphone feel.” Limit moving parts. Fewer characters and simpler motion improve fidelity, especially for physics or lip-sync scenes. Use Remix for branches. Keep versions clean—Remix instead of overwriting good takes. Storyboards Storyboards let you sketch out your video second by second, making it easier to bring more detailed ideas to life. Available first to ChatGPT Pro users, you can access storyboards by selecting ‘storyboard’ in the composer on sora.com . From there, you can build your video frame by frame from scratch —just like storyboards in Sora 1—or simply describe a scene , choose a duration, and let Sora generate a detailed storyboard you can edit. ChatGPT Pro users can generate videos of 25 seconds on web with storyboard. Stitch clips together Stitching lets you connect multiple clips into a single video so you can build a longer story and control each segment. In drafts, select the videos you want to stitch. Tap “stitch” when you’re ready. Preview the stitched video, then tap “stitch” again to start the stitching process. Your stitch will generate in drafts. Add a caption and select Post video to publish. Please note: stitched videos can be up to 60 seconds long (any combination of clip lengths). Edit clips in Sora Sora includes a built-in editor on iOS and web, with Android coming soon. You can use the editor to trim clips, stitch drafts together, reorder scenes, and keep iterating without leaving Sora. With the editor, you can: Trim clips frame by frame for more precise start and end points Stitch multiple clips into a single sequence Import videos directly from your drafts Reorder clips in your timeline Extend, reprompt, or remix clips to create new variations On web, you can reorder clips with drag and drop. On iOS, select a clip, tap Reorder , then drag and drop. Please note: undo and redo are available only during your current editor session. Usage limits Video generations contribute to the daily limit as follows: 10-second videos count as one video toward the daily limit. 15-second videos count as two videos toward daily limits. 25-second videos count as four videos toward the daily limit. How to set duration: On the Sora app, tap the dropdown under “Create video” at the top of the compose screen and select “Duration.” On sora.com, click the settings icon at the bottom left of the composer bar and select “Duration.” FAQ Can I import full videos to transform them? Not at launch. Use Remix on posts you see in Sora, or start from Text/Image. Can I choose 16:9? Yes. How do I get dialogue? Describe it in your prompt (“two friends chatting, natural café audio”) if you want to be specific; otherwise, the model will generate it automatically if it makes sense for the scene. For multi-speaker scenes, keep casts small and timing clear. Why does my download have a watermark? At launch, all exports include a moving visible watermark and C2PA provenance. How do usage limits work on the Sora app? Video creation in the Sora app uses a rolling 24-hour limit per account: each submitted request counts immediately, and there’s no midnight reset. You can submit again as soon as one of your recent submissions falls outside the last 24 hours. If you hit the cap, you’ll see an error like “You’ve already generated X videos in the last day” (i.e., in the last 24 hours). Can I download a Sora video without a watermark? In Sora 1 and Sora 2 , ChatGPT Pro users can download videos without a watermark when all of the following conditions are met: The video was generated from a text prompt The video does not depict a public figure The video does not use any characters When a watermark is added A watermark will appear on downloaded videos in the following situations: The video includes any character (including characters you created) The video uses someone else’s character The video depicts a public figure You are not subscribed to ChatGPT Pro The video uses characters in features such as Cast, Remix, or Extend In these cases, the downloaded video will include a watermark . Note that the Sora 1 web experience is actively being deprecated, and customers should transition away from Sora 1 web and look forward to Sora for Business. Expect more details in the coming months.
