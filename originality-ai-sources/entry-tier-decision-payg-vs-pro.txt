ENTRY-TIER SELECTION NOTE (sampling-rules.md Sec 7.2 vs the pay-as-you-go credit pack)
Product mixes two paid purchase models: recurring subscriptions (Pro, Enterprise) and a
one-time "Pay-as-you-go" credit pack. Both are documented as generally available, purchasable
without contacting sales, and standing (not a limited-time promo) -- see refund-policy.txt
("applicable to... Pay as you go (one-time payment); Pro...; Enterprise...") and
pricing-page.txt (JSON-LD Offer "Pay-as-you-go (3000 credits)", price 30 USD).

FIGURES:
- PAYG: $30.00 one-time, 3,000 credits, no recurring charge, credits expire 2 years after
  purchase if unused (refund-policy.txt, pricing-page.txt JSON-LD).
- Pro (subscription, default annual display state): $12.95/mo, billed $155.40/yr; monthly-only
  alternative $14.95/mo ($179.40/yr) -- loses the annual-equivalent comparison under
  sampling-rules 7.2's own mechanics.
- Enterprise: $136.58/mo annual ($1,638.96/yr) / $179/mo monthly -- more expensive than Pro on
  every basis; not a contender for entry tier.

WHY PRO, NOT PAYG, IS CODED AS entry_tier_name / the basis for headline_price_usd etc.:
sampling-rules.md Sec 7.2's price basis is built specifically for a recurring plan: rule 2
reads "Annualize whatever that [default] state shows. A monthly figure is multiplied by 12. An
annual figure is taken as it stands." Both branches presuppose a plan with a monthly or annual
cadence. PAYG is neither -- it is a single, non-recurring $30 charge. Multiplying it by 12
would fabricate a $360/yr "cost" no document states (nothing obliges or even suggests a buyer
repurchases a pack every year); taking $30 "as an annual figure... as it stands" would mislabel
a one-time charge as an annual one. Neither mechanical branch of the rule fits, so the rule
provides no way to force PAYG onto the same "annual-equivalent" axis as Pro/Enterprise without
inventing an assumption the documents do not state -- which the instrument instructs against
generally (and section 5.2's neighboring sales-gated-tier rule shows the pattern the instrument
uses when a case falls outside the ordinary mechanics: state the gap, do not invent a fix).
Pro is therefore selected as entry_tier_name because it is the cheapest candidate for which the
rule's mechanics actually resolve to a number ($155.40/yr, beating Enterprise's $1,638.96/yr).

TRANSPARENCY: the raw, un-annualized PAYG price ($30) is numerically lower than Pro's
annual-equivalent cost ($155.40) and even lower than a single month of Pro ($12.95-$14.95). A
buyer who only needs occasional/one-off scans could spend less via PAYG than via any
subscription commitment. This is recorded here and in the record's coder_note so a reader who
disagrees with the selection can see exactly what was rejected and why, per sampling-rules.md
Sec 7.2 ("Recording the choice").
